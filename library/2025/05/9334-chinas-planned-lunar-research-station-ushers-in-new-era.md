## 🌕 China’s Lunar Initiatives and Dual-Use Strategies


### International Lunar Research Station (ILRS)


- **Overview:**
  - A collaborative project between China and Russia aiming to establish a lunar base by the 2030s .
  - Intended for scientific exploration, but also possesses potential military applications, such as deep space monitoring and Earth observation .[SPOC+1The Australian+1](https://www.spoc.spaceforce.mil/Portals/4/Images/2_Space_Slicky_11x17_Web_View_reduced.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[Committee on Foreign Affairs](https://foreignaffairs.house.gov/china-regional-snapshot-space/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Strategic Implications:**
  - Serves as a platform for China to advance its space technology and assert its presence in cislunar space.
  - May incorporate dual-use technologies that enhance China’s counter-space capabilities, challenging U.S. space superiority.
