The **Satellite Deception Program** is a developing initiative within the **U.S. Space Force** aimed at enhancing **space-based resilience** by employing **counterspace deception tactics**. This program is designed to **mislead adversaries** about the location, function, and vulnerability of U.S. satellites, ensuring **operational security** in contested environments.


### Key Aspects of the Satellite Deception Program


- **Decoy Satellites & False Signatures**: Deploying **dummy satellites** or altering **electromagnetic signatures** to confuse enemy tracking systems.
- **Adaptive Orbital Maneuvers**: Using **unpredictable movement patterns** to evade detection and targeting.
- **Cyber & Electronic Countermeasures**: Implementing **signal spoofing** and **jamming techniques** to disrupt adversarial surveillance.


### Integration into the Golden Dome National Defense Strategy


The **Golden Dome** initiative is a **multi-layered missile defense shield** designed to protect the U.S. homeland from **ballistic, hypersonic, and cruise missile threats**2. The **Satellite Deception Program** integrates into this strategy by:


- **Enhancing Space-Based Missile Detection**: Ensuring **sensor networks remain operational** despite adversarial interference.
- **Strengthening Resilience Against ASAT Threats**: Protecting **critical satellites** from kinetic and cyber-based attacks.
- **Supporting Global Data Fusion**: Maintaining **secure communications** for missile tracking and defense coordination.
