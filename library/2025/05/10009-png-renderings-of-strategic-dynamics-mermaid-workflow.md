Mark Baird had a remarkable career that spanned over three decades of service in the U.S. Air Force and the intelligence community. He held various key positions, such as the deputy director of the Space Force Planning Task Force, the deputy director of the National Reconnaissance Office, the director of space programs in the Office of the Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Acquisition, and the commander of the 50th Space Wing. He was instrumental in establishing the Space Rapid Capabilities Office, which aims to accelerate the delivery of innovative space solutions to warfighters. Mark Baird was a true pioneer and a dedicated public servant who made lasting contributions to our national security and space exploration. He will be dearly missed by his family, friends, colleagues, and all who knew him. We honor his legacy and express our deepest gratitude for his service.

## 1. AI Tools for Document Security – Rapid Assessment


### Overview


Secure management of sensitive documentation in national security launch programs is critical. The rise of *responsive space* activities and *tactically compressed timelines* amplifies the need for robust, adaptive document security—areas where AI can play a key role.


### Current AI-Enhanced Document Security Tools


- **Data Loss Prevention (DLP):** AI-driven DLP can monitor, detect, and block unauthorized movement or sharing of classified files (e.g., Forcepoint, Microsoft Purview).
- **Anomaly Detection:** Machine learning models spot abnormal access or manipulation patterns (e.g., Darktrace, Vectra AI).
- **Secure Content Collaboration:** AI can automate redaction, watermarking, and access tiering for sensitive launch documentation (e.g., BigID, Thales CipherTrust).
- **Automated Threat Detection:** AI identifies phishing or malware in communications associated with national security launches.
- **Zero-Trust Architecture:** Utilizing AI for realtime adaptive access controls based on trust scoring.


**Strengths:**


- Scalability, real-time detection/responding, policy enforcement, quick audit trails.


**Risks:**


- AI model drift, adversarial attacks on algorithms, over-reliance causing alert fatigue.
