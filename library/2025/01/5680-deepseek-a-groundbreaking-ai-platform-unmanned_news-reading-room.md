**The Zhiyuan Institute, also known as the Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence (BAAI), is a prominent Chinese non-profit AI research laboratory. BAAI is dedicated to advancing AI research and fostering collaboration between academia and industry. It focuses on long-term research in AI fundamentals and aims to nurture top talent in the field.

BAAI’s founding members include leading AI companies, universities, and research institutes. The institute frequently releases new AI models and open-source code to help achieve its goals. BAAI is recognized as one of the pre-eminent AI research institutes in China.**
