## Fusion Dashboard or Q&A Use


### Potential Questions That the System Can Answer:


- **“Did Operation Heatwave cause a sustained drop in overdoses?”** **A:** No. The drop in available drugs was brief; overdose rates rebounded as DTOs rotated in new managers.
- **“Do NYC sanctuary policies affect DTO disruptions?”** **A:** Yes. By limiting ICE cooperation, DTOs can rotate personnel into distribution roles, maintaining market operations after raids.
- **“What is the friction between local and federal policy?”** **A:** Federal task forces can enable temporary enforcement; however, without ICE detainer follow-through, DTO personnel are soon replaced.


### Visualization Suggestions:


- **Timeline graphs:** Overlay enforcement events, policy changes, and OD clusters by date/location.
- **Network chart:** Show DTO hub, enforcement impacts, policy frictions, mapping resilience loops (arrest, replacement, re-emergence).
- **Alert triggers:** If (high enforcement + sanctuary friction) && (OD cluster count ↑), push “market restoration risk” alert.
