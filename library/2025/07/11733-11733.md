Bondi’s remarks, as Attorney General, underscore the seriousness of this issue, particularly highlighting the role of border security and law enforcement in combating the crisis.


1. West coast entry and distribution


- **Left Nodes (Cities):** San Francisco, Los Angeles
- **Right Nodes (States):** California, Arizona
- **Flow Band Themes:** Fentanyl enters the US via border states like California and Arizona, often originating from Mexico. Major cities like San Francisco and Los Angeles serve as key distribution hubs. Law enforcement efforts, including DEA operations, focus on intercepting these drugs at the border and within these cities. Forensic identification of analogs, coupled with data from crime labs and testing positivity rates, helps map the spread of fentanyl and its variants.


2. Eastern U.S. distribution


- **Left Nodes (Cities):** Philadelphia, Bronx (New York City)
- **Right Nodes (States):** Pennsylvania, Alabama, Texas
- **Flow Band Themes:** These cities are implicated in fentanyl trafficking, with reports of heroin/fentanyl mixtures and overdose deaths. Law enforcement activity, including DEA emergency scheduling and local crime lab data, tracks the presence of fentanyl and its analogs. Bondi mentioned busting a trafficking ring that had a presence in six states, and some reports mention Texas and Alabama in this context, highlighting the wider reach of these networks.
