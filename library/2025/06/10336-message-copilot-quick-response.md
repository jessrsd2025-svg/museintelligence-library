### 🕰️ Historical Background of the IRGC


- **1979**: Founded by Ayatollah Khomeini after the Islamic Revolution to protect the new regime and counterbalance the regular military (Artesh), which was seen as loyal to the Shah2.
- **1980–1988**: Played a pivotal role in the Iran–Iraq War, evolving from a paramilitary force into a parallel military institution with its own ground, naval, and air branches.
- **1985**: Formation of the **Quds Force**, tasked with extraterritorial operations and support for proxy groups like Hezbollah.
- **1990s–2000s**: Expanded into Iran’s political and economic spheres, gaining influence under Supreme Leader Khamenei and President Ahmadinejad3.
- **2010s**: Became central to Iran’s foreign policy, supporting militias in Syria, Iraq, Lebanon, and Yemen. The U.S. designated the IRGC as a terrorist organization in 20192.
- **2020**: Qassem Soleimani, head of the Quds Force, was killed in a U.S. drone strike, marking a major leadership loss.
- **2025**: Multiple senior IRGC leaders, including Commander-in-Chief Hossein Salami, were killed in Israeli airstrikes amid escalating conflict3.
