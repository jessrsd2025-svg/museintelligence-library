**Next-Generation Retrieval: GraphRAG & PathRAG**

**Knowledge retrieval extends beyond embeddings alone to encompass relational and interconnected elements. Traditional vector embedding approaches often yield semantically similar results but lack contextual relational clarity. The introduction of graph-based retrieval mechanisms (GraphRAG) improves this by indexing and organizing retrieved data as interconnected entities (nodes) and relationships (edges).**
