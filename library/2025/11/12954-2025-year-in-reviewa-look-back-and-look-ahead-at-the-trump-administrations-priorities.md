**🚀 Policy Update: Hardcoding the Future of U.S. Digital Finance & AI**


The 119th Congress and the White House have moved decisively to stabilize the “Wild West” of crypto while centralizing AI oversight. Here is what you need to know about the current regulatory landscape:


**1. The GENIUS Act: Setting the Gold Standard for Stablecoins**


The **Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins Act (GENIUS Act)**, sponsored by **Senator Bill Hagerty (R-TN)**, is now Public Law 119-27. This legislation moves stablecoins from a patchwork of state rules into a comprehensive federal framework.


• **The Reserve Requirement:** To protect consumers and ensure financial stability, issuers are mandated to hold **at least $1 of permitted, low-risk reserves for every $1 of stablecoins issued**,. This ensures that “payment stablecoins” remain redeemable at a fixed value, effectively backing the digital dollar with high-quality assets,.


**2. The 2025 AI Executive Order: National Unity over Fragmentation**


Issued on December 11, 2025, the administration’s AI Executive Order (EO) establishes a centralized “command and control” structure for artificial intelligence.


• **Primary Objectives:**


◦ **National Uniformity:** It creates a **single national AI regulation framework** designed to **preempt conflicting state laws**, providing a clear, singular path for developers in corridors like NYC and Silicon Valley,.


◦ **Enforcement & Litigation:** The EO establishes an **AI Litigation Task Force** and leverages **federal funding** to ensure strict compliance with national standards.


◦ **Innovation Control:** It mandates an EO registry for AI model deployment in exchanges and ensures clearinghouses align their risk models with federal standards,.

### 🧾 What the GENIUS Act Does


The GENIUS Act establishes the **first comprehensive federal regulatory framework for “payment stablecoins”** — digital assets designed for payments or settlement and redeemable at a fixed value (e.g., $1). [Congress.gov+1](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12553?utm_source=chatgpt.com)


#### Key Provisions Include:


- **Definition of Payment Stablecoins:** Digital assets issued for payments or settlement and redeemable at a predetermined fixed amount. [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12553?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Reserve Requirements:** Stablecoin issuers must hold at least $1 of permitted (low-risk) reserves for every $1 of stablecoins issued — intended to strengthen stability and consumer protection. [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12553?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Prohibitions on Misrepresentation:** It’s unlawful to claim stablecoins are backed by the full faith and credit of the United States or are FDIC/NCUA insured unless issued under this Act. Violations carry civil and criminal penalties. [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/1582/text?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Governance and Compliance:** Restrictions on individuals convicted of certain financial crimes serving as officers or directors of stablecoin issuers. [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/1582/text?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Federal and State Roles:** The Act opens a pathway for dual federal and certified state supervision of payment stablecoin issuers. [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2025/09/the-genius-acts-stablecoin-regulatory-scheme-promotes-uniformity-but-may-fall-short?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
