**Since 2019, significant political shifts have occurred in both the United States and Italy.**


During the signing of the MoU, Donald Trump was the President of the US, and his “America First” policy influenced transatlantic relations. Conversely, Joe Biden’s administration has endeavored to restore alliances, albeit with a protectionist approach. Similarly, Italy has seen changes in its domestic politics, cycling through various governments.


The political landscapes of the United States and Italy have witnessed considerable changes since 2019, influencing not only domestic policies but also the dynamics of transatlantic relations. The signing of the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) occurred during a period marked by President Donald Trump’s “America First” policy, which brought a degree of uncertainty to the longstanding partnership between the two nations. This approach had a palpable impact on transatlantic ties, challenging the conventional alignment on various international issues.


With the transition to President Joe Biden’s administration, there has been a concerted effort to mend and reinforce alliances, including the relationship with Italy. Despite upholding certain protectionist measures, the Biden administration has signaled a clear departure from its predecessor’s stance by emphasizing the importance of international cooperation and alliance-building.


Italy’s political scene has been equally dynamic, with the country navigating through a series of government transitions. The political turmoil and the emergence of new leadership have brought about shifts in Italy’s approach to both domestic and international affairs. The appointment of Mario Draghi, the former European Central Bank governor, as Italy’s Prime Minister marked a significant turn towards a more unified and stable government, expected to align closely with the economic and financial mainstream and the new direction of the US administration.


These political shifts have underscored the resilience and adaptability of the US-Italy relationship. As both nations continue to address global challenges such as the support for Ukraine, sustainable development, and energy security, their partnership remains a cornerstone of transatlantic cooperation. The shared values and principles of democracy, freedom, and human rights continue to form the bedrock of this enduring alliance.


As Italy assumes the G7 rotating presidency in 2024, the spotlight is on how it will navigate its role in shaping the agenda for global economic governance. The US-Italy partnership is poised to play a pivotal role in addressing contemporary challenges, leveraging their strong economic ties and strategic cooperation to promote peace, security, and prosperity on both sides of the Atlantic.


The future of US-Italian relations will likely be influenced by the ongoing political developments within both countries. As they strive to balance national interests with collective goals, the strength of their partnership will be tested and, potentially, reinforced. The transatlantic bond, with its rich history and shared aspirations, stands as a testament to the ability of nations to evolve and collaborate amidst changing political tides.

1. Memorandum of Understanding (MoU): In 2019, Italy entered into an MoU with China, officially joining the Belt and Road Initiative (BRI). The purpose of this agreement was to foster collaboration under the auspices of the Silk Road Economic Belt and the 21st Century Maritime Silk Road Initiative. Nevertheless, the initial MoU expired in March 2024..
2. **Dilemma and Decision**: Italy now faces a dilemma: whether to renew its participation in the BRI or withdraw. [The decision-making process has been influenced by shifts in both US and Italian politics since 2019](https://www.csis.org/analysis/italy-withdraws-chinas-belt-and-road-initiative)[1](https://www.csis.org/analysis/italy-withdraws-chinas-belt-and-road-initiative). [Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni’s administration has signaled a potential shift away from the BRI, emphasizing stronger economic ties with the US rather than China](https://www.csis.org/analysis/italy-withdraws-chinas-belt-and-road-initiative)[1](https://www.csis.org/analysis/italy-withdraws-chinas-belt-and-road-initiative).

Türkiye’s Growing Role in BRICS: A Strategic Shift Towards a Multipolar World


Türkiye’s interest in the BRICS group, comprising Brazil, Russia, India, China, and South Africa, marks a significant shift in the global economic and political landscape. As the world gravitates towards a multipolar order, Türkiye’s potential cooperation with BRICS could serve as a pivotal bridge between diverse geopolitical spheres.


The BRICS nations, which collectively hold a substantial share of the global GDP, are increasingly seen as a counterbalance to the Western-centric world order. Led by the economic powerhouses of Russia and China, BRICS is exploring alternatives to established Western financial systems, such as developing a substitute for SWIFT and establishing the New Development Bank, which could challenge the dominance of the US dollar and provide a shield against Western sanctions.


Türkiye’s economy, while distinct from the BRICS in several aspects, shares common aspirations for growth and autonomy in the global economy. Despite facing key economic challenges similar to those of BRICS countries, such as regional disparities, growing inequalities, and significant levels of unemployment and poverty, Türkiye’s economy is dynamic and evolving. Its strategic location and robust trade relations position it as a valuable partner for BRICS, potentially enhancing the bloc’s economic diversity and resilience.


Domestically, Türkiye’s diplomatic maneuvers, such as its support for Palestinian statehood and its stance on the situation in Gaza, reflect its independent foreign policy approach. This aligns with BRICS’ efforts to influence global politics and offer alternatives to Western hegemony.


Expert opinions suggest that Türkiye’s inclusion in BRICS could catalyze economic and geopolitical shifts, contributing significantly to the group’s objectives. However, Türkiye’s cooperation with BRICS is not without its challenges. There are concerns about how this partnership might affect Türkiye’s relations with the West and whether internal opposition within Türkiye could impact its foreign policy decisions.


As BRICS continues to expand, welcoming new members from the global South, its future influence on the world stage is poised to grow. The group must navigate internal divisions and consensus-building challenges, but the potential for a more balanced global order is on the horizon.


In conclusion, Türkiye’s engagement with BRICS underscores a strategic diversification in international alliances, reflecting the country’s pursuit of a nuanced and multi-dimensional foreign policy. This move could enhance Türkiye’s economic prospects and diplomatic leverage, contributing to the shaping of a more multipolar and inclusive world order. The implications of such a partnership will be closely watched by global observers as it unfolds in the coming years.

> Imagination is more important than knowledge. Knowledge is limited. Imagination encircles the world. — Albert Einstein


Albert Einstein’s assertion that “Imagination is more important than knowledge” highlights the boundless potential of creative thought as a driving force in transcending the limitations of existing knowledge. In this quote, Einstein emphasizes that while knowledge is crucial and provides the foundation of understanding, it is inherently limited to what is already known and understood. On the other hand, imagination plays a pivotal role in expanding these boundaries, allowing us to envision possibilities beyond current realities. Through the lens of imagination, scientific breakthroughs are conceptualized, societal changes are envisioned, and the arts explore new expressions. This perspective invites us to consider imagination not just as a complement to knowledge, but as an essential component of innovation and progress that encircles the globe with endless possibilities.
