## Fleet Marine Force Combat Operation Insignia


The Fleet Marine Force Combat Operation Insignia (also known as a globe device) is a bronze award device that signifies participation in specific campaigns as designated by the Secretary of the Navy. They are commonly awarded to hospital corpsmen, chaplains, and other non-combat roles who have done their duty in a combat zone. The Fleet Marine Force Combat Operation Insignia is centered on the ribbon and is the highest priority making all other devices on the ribbons shifted to the right.This device is in a 5/16″ size.It comes in Bronze.[Military-Ranks.org](https://www.military-ranks.org/)


- The Fleet Marine Force Combat Operation Insignia is awarded to U.S. Navy sailors attached to FMF units during combat operations. Notable recipients include Medal of Honor recipients, Navy chaplains, and hospital corpsmen.
- Specific campaigns associated with this insignia include significant battles and operations in which the FMF was heavily engaged, such as the Invasion of Inchon (1950) during the Korean War and the Battle of Hue (1968) in Vietnam.
- To qualify for this award, sailors must be assigned to FMF units and participate in active combat operations, demonstrating exemplary service and commitment under combat conditions.
- In Operation Iraqi Freedom (2003-2011), the FMF played a crucial role in the initial invasion and subsequent stabilization efforts. The 1st Marine Expeditionary Force (MEF) spearheaded the advance into Iraq, capturing key objectives and providing essential security and support during the occupation.


**Humanitarian and Disaster Relief Missions:**


- The FMF plays a role in disaster relief, collaborating with the U.S. Navy on humanitarian assistance and disaster relief activities globally.
- The Department of Defense (DoD) has policies for foreign humanitarian assistance and disaster relief, including funding through the Overseas Humanitarian, Disaster, and Civic Aid appropriation.


**Recent Technological Advancements:**


- The FMF is incorporating new technologies to enhance operational effectiveness. These advancements include the use of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) for reconnaissance and surveillance, improved communication systems for better coordination, and advanced armored vehicles for increased protection.
- Development and integration of cyber warfare capabilities and electronic warfare technologies are also enhancing the FMF’s ability to operate in modern conflict environments.
- Enhanced training simulators and virtual reality systems are being used to improve combat readiness and tactical decision-making.


The FMF’s adaptability and commitment significantly contribute to U.S. national security. 🌊🌎🇺🇸
