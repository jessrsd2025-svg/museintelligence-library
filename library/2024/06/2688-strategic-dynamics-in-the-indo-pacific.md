1. **Bipartisan Task Force**:
  - The House Committee on Natural Resources established the bipartisan Indo-Pacific Task Force in June 2023.
  - Chaired by Congresswoman Aumua Amata Coleman Radewagen, it focused on U.S. territories and the Freely Associated States (FAS) in the Indo-Pacific.
  - [The task force examined measures to counter China’s influence in the region and addressed COFA renewal](https://naturalresources.house.gov/uploadedfiles/iptf_findings.pdf)[1](https://naturalresources.house.gov/uploadedfiles/iptf_findings.pdf).
2. **China’s Influence and Concerns**:
  - China’s increased presence in the Indo-Pacific raised concerns.
  - The task force explored ways to maintain U.S. interests and respond to coercive actions by China.
  - [Economic aid and infrastructure projects by China were closely monitored](https://naturalresources.house.gov/uploadedfiles/iptf_findings.pdf)[1](https://naturalresources.house.gov/uploadedfiles/iptf_findings.pdf).
