1. **Purpose and Scope**:
  - The committee focuses on safety and security aspects across all OpenAI projects.
  - It evaluates risks, potential harms, and mitigation strategies related to AI systems.
2. **Composition**:
  - The committee is led by prominent individuals, including Bret Taylor (Chair), Adam D’Angelo, Nicole Seligman, and CEO Sam Altman.
  - Their expertise spans technology, policy, and safety.
3. **Responsibilities**:
  - Reviewing safety practices: The committee assesses OpenAI’s safety protocols and recommends improvements.
  - Security considerations: It examines vulnerabilities, adversarial attacks, and potential misuse.
  - Ethical alignment: Ensuring AI systems align with human values and societal well-being.
4. **Timeline**:
  - Over the next 90 days, the committee will actively contribute to OpenAI’s safety efforts.
