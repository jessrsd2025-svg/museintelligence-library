**Intelligence Community Integration**:


DNI Haines released the **2023 National Intelligence Strategy (NIS)**, which outlines six goals reflecting key elements of the current strategic environment:


1. **Strategic Competition**: Focusing on competition between the U.S., **China**, and **Russia**.
2. **Emerging Technologies**: Recognizing their importance to national security.
3. **Supply Chains and Economic Statecraft**: Addressing vulnerabilities.
4. **Influence of Sub-National and Non-State Actors**: Acknowledging their increasing impact.
5. [**Shared Global Challenges**: Including climate change and health security](https://www.hstoday.us/subject-matter-areas/intelligence/director-haines-releases-2023-national-intelligence-strategy-for-the-ic-community/)
