By establishing a strong infrastructure and supply chain for commercial ships, China has laid the groundwork for military shipbuilding as well.


In 1993, China held a mere **3% market share** in global commercial shipbuilding. [Today, it constructs nearly half of the world’s commercial ships and receives over **55% of new orders**](https://www.usni.org/magazines/proceedings/2024/march/how-overcome-chinas-maritime-industrial-overmatch)
