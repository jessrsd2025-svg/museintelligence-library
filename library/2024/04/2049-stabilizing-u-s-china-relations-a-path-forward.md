**Evidence of Election Interference**


- U.S. Secretary of State Antony Blinken recently visited China and raised concerns about Beijing’s attempts to influence the 2024 U.S. [elections](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2024/apr/26/antony-blinken-beijing-visiit-china-us-relations-foreign-minister-wang-yi).
- Blinken emphasized that any interference by China in U.S. elections is unacceptable and must be addressed promptly.
- While President Xi Jinping assured that China would not interfere, the U.S. remains vigilant.


In summary, while there are areas of cooperation, challenges remain, especially concerning China’s support for Russia’s defense industry. Stabilizing U.S.-China relations requires addressing these issues while maintaining open channels of communication. [Geopolitical implications, technological advancements, and human rights concerns also play crucial roles in shaping the path forward.](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2024/apr/26/antony-blinken-beijing-visiit-china-us-relations-foreign-minister-wang-yi)
