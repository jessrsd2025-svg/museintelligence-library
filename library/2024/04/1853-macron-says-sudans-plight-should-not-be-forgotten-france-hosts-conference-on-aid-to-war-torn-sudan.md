Humanitarian workers in Sudan face **significant challenges** while providing aid and relief to those affected by the conflict. Here are some of the key difficulties they encounter:


1. **Security Risks**:
  - **Armed Violence**: The ongoing conflict poses **direct threats** to humanitarian workers. [Armed groups, militias, and violence in conflict zones make it dangerous to operate](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses)[1](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses).
  - [**Bureaucratic Impediments**: Obtaining necessary **visas** and permits can be cumbersome due to bureaucratic hurdles and security concerns](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses)[2](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/analysis/2023/06/29/obstacles-aid-pile-sudans-conflict-rages).
  - [**Operational Interference**: Authorities sometimes **restrict or interfere** with humanitarian operations, hindering access to affected populations](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses)[2](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/analysis/2023/06/29/obstacles-aid-pile-sudans-conflict-rages).
2. **Financial Constraints**:
  - **Shattered Banking System**: The Sudanese banking system has been severely affected by the conflict. [Many humanitarian workers **cannot collect salaries** due to financial instability](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses)[1](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses).
3. **Job Losses and Unpaid Salaries**:
  - **Program Suspensions**: International organizations have suspended programs in areas deemed unsafe. [As a result, more than **100 Sudanese workers** with Save the Children recently lost their jobs](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses)[1](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses).
  - [**Unpaid Salaries**: Some workers continue to provide aid without receiving their salaries, impacting their livelihoods](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses)[1](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses).
4. **Reduced Humanitarian Presence**:
  - The number of NGOs and aid agencies operating in Sudan has **decreased significantly** since the conflict began. [This reduction affects the overall capacity to deliver aid](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses)[1](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses).


In summary, humanitarian workers in Sudan grapple with security risks, financial constraints, job losses, and operational challenges. [Despite these difficulties, they remain committed to reaching those in need](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses)[1](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2023/08/01/exclusive-sudanese-aid-workers-face-hundreds-job-losses)[2](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/analysis/2023/06/29/obstacles-aid-pile-sudans-conflict-rages).
