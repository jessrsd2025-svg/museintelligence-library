Secretary of Defense **Lloyd J. Austin III** recently addressed the **Ukraine Defense Industrial Base Conference**, emphasizing the unwavering commitment of the United States to stand with the people of Ukraine. He acknowledged the remarkable resilience of the Ukrainian people in the face of **Putin’s tragic and unnecessary war**. Here are some key points from his speech:


1. **Ukrainian Resilience**: Secretary Austin praised the courage and skill of **Ukraine’s troops**, who have demonstrated incredible bravery while defending their country. Their unwavering spirit has been instrumental in reclaiming more than half of the territory occupied by Russia since the invasion began in February 2022.
2. **Global Coalition**: The United States has forged an extraordinary coalition of **50 allies and partners worldwide** to support Ukraine. Together, they have committed substantial security assistance—over **$44 billion** from the U.S. and an additional **$36 billion** from other nations. Several countries have contributed even more than the United States relative to their GDP.
3. **Clear Objective**: President Biden’s objective is clear: a **free and sovereign Ukraine** capable of defending itself today and deterring future Russian aggression. This commitment extends beyond the immediate conflict, emphasizing long-term support for Ukraine.
4. **Technology and Defense**: Secretary Austin highlighted the partnership between the **Department of Defense** and the **Commerce Department**. Their collaboration plays a crucial role in providing key weapons systems and capabilities to Ukrainian fighters.


In summary, Secretary Austin’s words underscore the importance of unity, resilience, and global cooperation in defending Ukraine’s sovereignty and self-determination. [The ongoing efforts aim not only to repel Russian forces today but also to build a strong future force for Ukraine’s security](https://www.defense.gov/News/Speeches/Speech/Article/3609023/remarks-by-secretary-of-defense-lloyd-j-austin-iii-at-the-ukraine-defense-indus/)
