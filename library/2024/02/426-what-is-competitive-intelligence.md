Iran has acknowledged the involvement of the IRGC-QF in the Iraq and Syria conflicts, and the IRGC-QF is Iran’s primary mechanism for cultivating and supporting terrorist activity abroad.

Iran has a long history of supporting various armed factions in the Middle East, particularly those opposing Israel and the United States. Some of the prominent groups backed by Iran include:


1. **Hezbollah** in Lebanon
2. **Hamas** and **Islamic Jihad** in Palestine
3. **The Houthis** in Yemen
4. Various **Shia militias** in Iraq and Syria


Iran provides these groups with financial, military, and ideological assistance, viewing them as allies and proxies to advance its interests and influence in the region. However, this support has also drawn criticism and condemnation from other countries. For instance:


- **Saudi Arabia**, Iran’s main regional rival, accuses Iran of destabilizing the region and interfering in other countries’ internal affairs.
- The **United States**, considering Iran a state sponsor of terrorism, has imposed sanctions on Iran and its proxies and threatened military action over Iran’s nuclear program.
- **Israel**, perceiving Iran as an existential threat, has conducted airstrikes and covert operations against Iran and its allies, warning of potential war if Iran acquires nuclear weapons.
