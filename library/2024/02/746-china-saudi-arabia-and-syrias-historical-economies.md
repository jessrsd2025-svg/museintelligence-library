SAFE, the State Administration of Foreign Exchange of China, manages its foreign exchange reserves by adhering to principles of safety, liquidity, and maintaining value. While specific strategies can vary, common practices include diversifying investments across different currencies and asset classes, and using various financial instruments to manage risks and returns. [The reserves are typically invested in secure assets like government bonds and held in multiple currencies to spread risk and ensure liquidity](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/international-market-operations/foreign-reserves-management).


As for Pan Gongsheng, he is a prominent Chinese economist who has been serving as the Governor of the People’s Bank of China (PBC) since July 2023. He also holds the position of the Director of SAFE. Pan Gongsheng has a rich background in economics, with a Ph.D. from Renmin University of China, and post-doctoral research at Cambridge University. He’s known for his work in commercial banking and his significant contributions to monetary policy in China. [Notably, he has been involved in managing internet financial risks, including regulations around cryptocurrencies in China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan_Gongsheng)[5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan_Gongsheng)[6](https://www.bbc.com/news/business-66309002)[7](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/politics/article/3226242/china-names-forex-regulatory-head-pan-gongsheng-communist-party-chief-central-bank)[8](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202307/1295052.shtml).
