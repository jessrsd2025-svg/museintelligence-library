[The Middle East Crisis, particularly with Iran, involves complex defense strategies and regional dynamics. Iran’s approach has been shaped by its desire to establish a regional order that counters the influence of external powers like the United States](https://peacediplomacy.org/2022/11/11/the-iranian-regional-deterrence-strategy-in-the-new-middle-east/)[1](https://peacediplomacy.org/2022/11/11/the-iranian-regional-deterrence-strategy-in-the-new-middle-east/). This strategy includes:


[**Counter-Containment**: Iran has developed strategies to overcome the limits imposed by the US containment policy, which includes military, security, and economic dimensions](https://peacediplomacy.org/2022/11/11/the-iranian-regional-deterrence-strategy-in-the-new-middle-east/)[1](https://peacediplomacy.org/2022/11/11/the-iranian-regional-deterrence-strategy-in-the-new-middle-east/).


[**Deterrence Doctrine**: Since the 1980s, Iran has focused on developing long-range ballistic missiles and asymmetric warfare capabilities as a deterrent, especially against perceived threats from the US and Israel](https://www.mei.edu/publications/upgrading-irans-military-doctrine-offensive-forward-defense)[2](https://www.mei.edu/publications/upgrading-irans-military-doctrine-offensive-forward-defense).


[**Regional Alliances**: The formation of the “Axis of Resistance,” a coalition of states and movements across the Middle East, aims to deter American and Israeli threats](https://peacediplomacy.org/2022/11/11/the-iranian-regional-deterrence-strategy-in-the-new-middle-east/)[1](https://peacediplomacy.org/2022/11/11/the-iranian-regional-deterrence-strategy-in-the-new-middle-east/).


[Recent developments, such as the ongoing Vienna talks on the Iran Nuclear Deal and the emergence of the Abraham Accords, have further influenced the regional security architecture, with Iran seeking to adapt its foreign policy and defense strategy amid these changes](https://peacediplomacy.org/2022/11/11/the-iranian-regional-deterrence-strategy-in-the-new-middle-east/)[1](https://peacediplomacy.org/2022/11/11/the-iranian-regional-deterrence-strategy-in-the-new-middle-east/).


[Moreover, Iran’s unveiling of new air defense systems, like the Arman anti-ballistic missile system and the Azarakhsh low-altitude air defense system, reflects its commitment to enhancing its military capabilities in response to regional tensions](https://www.voanews.com/a/iran-reveals-air-defense-systems-as-middle-east-tensions-soar-/7491942.html)[3](https://www.voanews.com/a/iran-reveals-air-defense-systems-as-middle-east-tensions-soar-/7491942.html). [These systems are designed to identify and engage targets at significant distances and can handle multiple threats simultaneously](https://www.voanews.com/a/iran-reveals-air-defense-systems-as-middle-east-tensions-soar-/7491942.html)[3](https://www.voanews.com/a/iran-reveals-air-defense-systems-as-middle-east-tensions-soar-/7491942.html).


In summary, Iran’s defense strategy in the Middle East Crisis is characterized by a combination of counter-containment efforts, a deterrence doctrine, and regional alliances to bolster its security and regional influence in the face of external pressures and evolving geopolitical landscapes.
