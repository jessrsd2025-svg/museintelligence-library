After clearing the banks of the Siverskyi Donets-Donbas canal from enemy forces and entrenching in captured positions, the fighters of the brigade are starting a new stage and storming the approaches to the occupied village of Andriivka. The **205th Separate Cossack Motor Rifle Brigade (205 omsbr)** (Russian: 205-я отдельная мотострелковая казачья бригада) is a mechanized infantry brigade of the **Russian Ground Forces**. It is part of the **49th Combined Arms Army** and is based in **Budyonnovsk, Stavropol Krai**. [Formed in **1995** during the **First Chechen War**, this brigade has been involved in various Russian post-Soviet conflicts, including the **War of Dagestan**, **Second Chechen War**, the **Russo-Georgian War**, and the **2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/205th_Separate_Motor_Rifle_Brigade)[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/205th_Separate_Motor_Rifle_Brigade).


The **205th Brigade** was initially created by combining battalions and companies from other units. It included the **1387th**, **1393rd**, **1394th**, and **1396th Separate Motor Rifle Battalions**, as well as other supporting units. [Despite its planned base in Grozny and Shali, the brigade was continuously engaged in combat from its inception](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/205th_Separate_Motor_Rifle_Brigade)[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/205th_Separate_Motor_Rifle_Brigade).


[The **205th Separate Cossack Motor Rifle Brigade** has a rich history of service in various conflicts and remains an integral part of the **Russian Ground Forces**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/205th_Separate_Motor_Rifle_Brigade)
