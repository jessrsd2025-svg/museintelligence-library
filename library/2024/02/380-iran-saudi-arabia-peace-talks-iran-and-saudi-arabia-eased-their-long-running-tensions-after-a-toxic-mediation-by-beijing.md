[Oil revenues significantly impact governance and international relations, often leading to a phenomenon known as the ‘resource curse,’ where oil-rich nations tend to have less economic growth and democracy compared to those without abundant natural resources](https://now.tufts.edu/2023/10/04/why-democracy-so-elusive-oil-rich-middle-east)[1](https://now.tufts.edu/2023/10/04/why-democracy-so-elusive-oil-rich-middle-east)[2](https://www.weforum.org/agenda/2015/06/the-changing-geopolitics-of-oil-in-the-middle-east/)[3](https://www.mei.edu/publications/geopolitics-energy-transition-mena-mitigating-risks-exploring-new-opportunities).
