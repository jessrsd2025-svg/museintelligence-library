**The New York Times** reported that Israel was responsible for attacks on two major natural gas pipelines inside Iran. Although these attacks disrupted the flow of heat and cooking gas to provinces with millions of people, fortunately, there were no casualties. The incident marks an escalation in the ongoing shadow war between Israel and Iran. The enemy’s goal was to cut off gas supplies to large provinces, but this outcome was averted. [As tensions persist, the international community faces the challenge of curbing Iran’s actions to prevent further escalation](https://www.timesofisrael.com/report-iran-cautioned-hezbollah-not-to-spark-full-scale-war-with-israel/)[3](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/world/odds-of-israel-hezbollah-war-inevitable-experts-fear-totally-pessimistic/ar-BB1ivMY6).


In summary, Iran’s regional proxies are being cautioned, and Israel’s actions have escalated the conflict.
