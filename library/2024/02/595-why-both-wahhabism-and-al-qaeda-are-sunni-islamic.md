The **BBC documentary series “House of Saud: A Family at War”** provides an in-depth look at the challenges faced by the new Crown Prince of Saudi Arabia, **Mohammed bin Salman**. Let’s explore further:


1. **Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman (MBS):**
  - **Background**: MBS is a 32-year-old Saudi prince who has assumed significant power in the kingdom. He follows a lineage of Saudi rulers who wielded total authority domestically and often operated with relative impunity on the global stage.
  - **Different Approach**: Unlike his predecessors, MBS has distinct plans for Saudi Arabia. He has publicly committed to addressing corruption, extremism, and implementing social reforms within the country.
  - **Challenges**: However, Saudi Arabia finds itself at the center of the escalating Middle East crisis, raising questions about MBS’s ability to navigate these complexities.
  - **Moderate Islamism**: The documentary series explores whether this controversial state can collaborate with Western nations to promote moderate Islamism, given its pivotal role in regional affairs.
2. **Documentary Series Details**:
  - The BBC series travels across various locations, including **eastern Europe, India, Syria, Yemen,** and **inside Saudi Arabia**.
