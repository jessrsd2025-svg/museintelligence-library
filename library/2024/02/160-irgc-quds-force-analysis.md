Quds Force leadership axis of resistance


The “Axis of Resistance” refers to an alliance of state and non-state entities supported by Iran, primarily through the Quds Force, which is a branch of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC). The leadership of the Quds Force has been instrumental in coordinating this axis, which includes groups like Hezbollah, Hamas, and various Shia militias across the Middle East. The axis is united by a shared opposition to Western influence and Israel. The Quds Force provides military, financial, and strategic support to these groups, enhancing their operational capabilities. However, the autonomy of each group allows Iran plausible deniability for their actions, which serves Tehran’s strategic interests while avoiding direct conflict with powerful adversaries1234.
