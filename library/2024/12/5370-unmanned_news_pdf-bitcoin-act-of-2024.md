**The BITCOIN Act of 2024, officially titled the Boosting Innovation, Technology, and Competitiveness through Optimized Investment Nationwide Act of 2024, was introduced by Senator Cynthia Lummis. This bill aims to establish a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and outlines the acquisition and storage of Bitcoin by the U.S. government.**


**Key points of the BITCOIN Act of 2024 include:**


1. **Strategic Bitcoin Reserve**: The U.S. Treasury is required to purchase one million Bitcoins over a five-year period and hold them in a decentralized network of secure facilities across the United States.
2. **Long-term Storage**: All Bitcoins acquired under this bill must be held for at least 20 years unless used to retire outstanding federal debt.
3. **State Participation**: States may voluntarily store their Bitcoin holdings in the reserve in segregated accounts.
4. **Federal Reserve Involvement**: The bill reduces the total amount of U.S. dollars Federal Reserve banks may hold in surplus and requires them to remit a certain amount of net earnings annually to the purchase of Bitcoins.


**The bill is currently in the first stage of the legislative process, having been introduced and referred to the Senate Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.**
