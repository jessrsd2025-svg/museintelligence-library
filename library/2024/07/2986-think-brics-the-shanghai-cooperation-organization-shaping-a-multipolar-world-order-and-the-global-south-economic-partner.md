It is the world’s largest regional organization in terms of geographic scope and population, covering approximately 80% of the area of Eurasia[3] and 40% of the world population. As of 2023, its combined GDP based on PPP was around 32% of the world’s total.[4]

### Historical Background


The SCO was founded in 2001 by China and Russia, initially focusing on addressing security concerns in Central Asia. The organization emerged from the earlier “Shanghai Five” group, which included China, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and Tajikistan. With the inclusion of Uzbekistan in 2001, the group was renamed the Shanghai Cooperation Organization. Over time, the SCO’s mandate expanded beyond security issues to encompass a broader range of cooperative efforts, including economic, cultural, and humanitarian initiatives. The SCO has evolved into a comprehensive regional organization, providing a platform for dialogue and collaboration among its member states.


---


### Membership and Structure


The SCO comprises nine member states: China, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, India, Pakistan, and Iran. Additionally, the organization includes several observer states and dialogue partners, such as Turkey, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt. The organizational structure of the SCO includes key institutions like the Secretariat, based in Beijing, and the Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure (RATS), headquartered in Tashkent. These institutions facilitate the coordination of the SCO’s activities and initiatives, ensuring effective cooperation among its members.

### Core Objectives


#### Security and Stability


One of the primary objectives of the SCO is to ensure security and maintain stability across the vast Eurasian region. The organization addresses a wide range of security issues, including terrorism, separatism, and extremism. Through joint military exercises and intelligence sharing, the SCO enhances the collective security capabilities of its member states.


#### Counteracting Threats


The SCO actively works to counteract emerging challenges and threats in the region. This includes addressing issues such as drug trafficking, organized crime, and cyber threats. By pooling resources and expertise, the SCO aims to effectively tackle these challenges and enhance regional security.


#### Trade and Cooperation


In addition to security, the SCO promotes economic cooperation and trade among its member states. The organization facilitates cultural exchange and humanitarian cooperation, fostering stronger ties and mutual understanding. Initiatives like the SCO Development Bank and various economic corridors contribute to the economic integration of the region.

### Role in Shaping a Multipolar World Order


A multipolar world order refers to a global system where power is distributed among multiple major players, rather than being dominated by a single superpower. The SCO supports the establishment of such an order by fostering cooperation and collaboration among its member states. By promoting a more balanced distribution of influence, the SCO contributes to a more equitable and stable global order. Its efforts in enhancing regional cooperation and addressing shared challenges underscore its commitment to this vision.


---


### Key Initiatives and Achievements


The SCO has undertaken several major initiatives across various fields. In the realm of security, the organization has conducted numerous joint military exercises and established mechanisms for intelligence sharing. Economically, the SCO has promoted initiatives like the Belt and Road Initiative (BRI), which aims to enhance connectivity and trade in the region. Culturally, the SCO has facilitated numerous exchange programs and cultural events, fostering mutual understanding and cooperation among its member states.


---


### Challenges and Criticisms


Despite its achievements, the SCO faces several challenges. Internal issues, such as differing national interests and priorities among member states, can hinder effective cooperation. Externally, the organization faces criticism regarding its effectiveness and geopolitical motives. Some observers argue that the SCO primarily serves the strategic interests of its dominant members, China and Russia, rather than genuinely promoting regional cooperation.


---


### Future Prospects


Looking ahead, the SCO has the potential for further expansion and increased influence. Prospective new members, such as Afghanistan and Mongolia, could join the organization, enhancing its regional footprint. Additionally, the SCO aims to strengthen its partnerships with other regional and international organizations. The long-term goals of the SCO include promoting sustainable development, enhancing regional connectivity, and contributing to a more stable and multipolar world order.

### Conclusion


In summary, the Shanghai Cooperation Organization plays a crucial role in regional security, stability, and cooperation. By addressing shared challenges and promoting mutual understanding among its member states, the SCO contributes to shaping a world order where multiple powers coexist and collaborate.
