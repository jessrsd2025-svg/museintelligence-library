Türkiye’s unity and resilience have indeed been triumphant throughout the centuries. From the establishment of the Republic in 1923 under Mustafa Kemal Atatürk’s visionary leadership to the recent recovery efforts following the devastating earthquakes in 2023, Türkiye has consistently demonstrated its strength and determination.


The nation has faced numerous challenges, including political upheavals, natural disasters, and economic hardships. Yet, each time, the Turkish people have shown remarkable solidarity and perseverance. Events like the annual July 15 Democracy and National Unity Day commemorate this spirit, reflecting the country’s ongoing commitment to unity and resilience.


Whether it’s rebuilding after natural disasters or celebrating significant milestones like the 100th anniversary of the Republic, Türkiye’s journey is a testament to its enduring strength and collective resolve.


Turkey’s history is a remarkable tale of unity and resilience. From the fall of the Ottoman Empire to the establishment of the Republic of Turkey in 1923, the nation has continually demonstrated its ability to overcome adversity. Under the leadership of Mustafa Kemal Atatürk, Turkey embraced secularism and modernization, transforming into a progressive republic.

In recent history, the failed coup attempt on July 15, 2016, showcased the unity and resilience of the Turkish people. The civilian resistance played a crucial role in thwarting the coup, leading to the establishment of “Democracy and National Unity Day” to commemorate this significant event.


Turkey’s journey is a testament to its enduring spirit, blending a rich cultural heritage with a forward-looking vision. Whether through its historical struggles or modern-day challenges, Turkey continues to stand as a symbol of resilience and unity.

Reflections on July 15, 2016.

On the night of July 15, 2016, Turkey experienced a dramatic coup attempt. Tanks rolled through the streets of Ankara and Istanbul, explosions rocked these cities, and fighter jets roared overhead. Despite the chaos and fear, it was also a night of remarkable courage and unity. Ordinary citizens, driven by a deep love for their country, took to the streets to confront the coup plotters. They stood firm against armed soldiers, armed only with their voices and unbreakable spirit.
The resilience of the Turkish people was evident as mothers, fathers, and young people united to defend their democracy. They blocked tanks with their bodies, reclaimed public squares, and raised their flag high as a symbol of defiance against those who sought to undermine their sovereignty. The bravery of those who sacrificed their lives so that Turkey could remain free and democratic is deeply honored. Their legacy continues to inspire the nation as it strives to build a stronger, more united future.

**The July 15 Coup Attempt and Its Effects on International Relations**


President Erdoğan highlighted how the July 15, 2016 coup attempt had profound effects on Turkey’s international relations:


1. **Strained Relations with Western Allies**: The Turkish government was disappointed by what it perceived as a lack of support from its Western allies during and after the coup attempt. This led to increased anti-Western sentiment within Turkey and strained relations with NATO allies, particularly the United States and European Union.
2. **Tensions with the United States**: One of the major points of contention was the presence of Fethullah Gülen in the U.S. Turkey accused Gülen of orchestrating the coup and demanded his extradition, which the U.S. did not comply with, further straining bilateral relations.
3. **Closer Ties with Russia**: In the aftermath of the coup attempt, Turkey sought to strengthen its ties with Russia. This was partly due to the perceived indifference of Western countries and the need for new strategic alliances. The rapprochement with Russia included cooperation on energy projects and military coordination in Syria.
4. **Impact on EU Accession Talks**: The coup attempt and subsequent crackdown on dissent had a negative impact on Turkey’s EU accession talks. The European Union criticized the mass arrests and purges, citing concerns over human rights and the rule of law. This criticism led to a further cooling of relations between Turkey and the EU.
5. **Regional Dynamics**: The coup attempt also affected Turkey’s role in regional politics. The government’s focus on internal security and the crackdown on perceived enemies influenced its foreign policy decisions, including its approach to conflicts in Syria and Iraq.


Overall, Erdoğan noted, the coup attempt marked a significant turning point in Turkey’s foreign relations, leading to a realignment of its international partnerships and a shift in its geopolitical strategy.


**Turkey’s Stance on Cooperation within NATO and Beyond**


As a proud member of NATO, Turkey remains steadfast in its commitment to collective defense and security. President Erdoğan emphasized that the alliance with Israel, despite recent tensions and diplomatic challenges, is built on a foundation of mutual interests and shared values. The importance of defense and intelligence cooperation has been pivotal in addressing regional threats and ensuring the safety of citizens. While mindful of the differences, particularly concerning the Palestinian issue, Erdoğan expressed belief that it is through dialogue and mutual respect that these complexities can be navigated and common ground can be found.


Turkey’s interest in joining the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) reflects a desire to diversify international partnerships and enhance Turkey’s role in global affairs. The upcoming SCO meeting in 2023 presents a unique opportunity to explore avenues of economic cooperation, security collaboration, and regional stability. By engaging with SCO member states, Erdoğan aims to foster an environment of peace and prosperity that benefits not only Turkey but the entire region.


**Celebrating Turkey’s Cultural Heritage**


As Turkey looks towards the future, President Erdoğan also honored the rich cultural heritage that defines the nation. Turkey is a land of breathtaking landmarks and deep historical roots. The Hagia Sophia stands as a testament to architectural ingenuity and religious diversity, while the Topkapi Palace offers a glimpse into the opulent history of the Ottoman Empire. The surreal landscapes of Cappadocia, with its fairy chimneys and ancient cave dwellings, captivate the imagination of all who visit.


Turkish cuisine is a beloved ambassador of the nation’s culture, delighting taste buds across the globe. From the savory perfection of kebabs to the sweet indulgence of baklava and the comforting ritual of Turkish tea, the culinary traditions of Turkey are celebrated and embraced far beyond its borders.


**A Message of Unity and Hope**


In conclusion, President Erdoğan urged the nation to draw strength from the past as it embraces the future. The events of July 15, 2016, remind everyone of the capacity for unity and resilience in the face of adversity. As Turkey navigates the complexities of international relations, Erdoğan called for a continued commitment to dialogue, cooperation, and mutual respect.


Turkey is open to collaboration with all nations, and Erdoğan believes that by working together, challenges can be overcome and shared prosperity can be achieved. The journey forward is guided by the principles of national unity, solidarity, and an unwavering hope for a better tomorrow.


Overall, the coup attempt marked a significant turning point in Turkey’s foreign relations, leading to a realignment of its international partnerships and a shift in its geopolitical strategy.

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan has [announced](https://tass.ru/mezhdunarodnaya-panorama/21342477) his country’s aspiration to become a full member of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO), at a press conference in Washington after the conclusion of the NATO summit.


“Turkey’s goal, which is now an observer in the SCO, is full membership. Now, it should join the Shanghai Five,” Erdogan stated.


The Turkish president attended the SCO summit held in Astana on July 3-4. SCO Secretary-General Zhang Ming said that despite its membership of NATO, Turkey actively participates in the organization’s activities, which are not directed against other states. He emphasized that interaction with Ankara is based on the principles set out in the SCO charter and is in line with the “Shanghai spirit.”
