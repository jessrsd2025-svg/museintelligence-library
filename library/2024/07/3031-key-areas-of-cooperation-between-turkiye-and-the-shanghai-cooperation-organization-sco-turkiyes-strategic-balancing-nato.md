Türkiye’s strategic engagement with the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) has evolved significantly over the years, reflecting the country’s broader foreign policy objectives and its desire to expand influence across multiple international platforms. Let’s delve into key aspects:


Türkiye’s cooperation with the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) encompasses several key areas that are vital for both Türkiye and the SCO member states. These areas of cooperation facilitate mutual benefits and contribute to regional stability and development.


### Security Collaboration


One of the primary areas of cooperation is in the realm of security. Türkiye has been actively involved in joint anti-terrorism exercises, which are critical for maintaining regional security. These exercises help in the preparation and coordination of counter-terrorism efforts among member states. Additionally, Türkiye participates in intelligence sharing within the Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure (RATS), which enhances the collective security mechanisms of the SCO.


### Economic and Infrastructure Development


Economic collaboration is another significant area of cooperation. Türkiye engages in infrastructure projects that align with the SCO’s objectives of improving connectivity and economic integration among its members. These projects include the development of transportation networks, energy pipelines, and other initiatives that facilitate trade and investment opportunities.


### Cultural Exchanges and Humanitarian Aid


Cultural and humanitarian cooperation also plays an essential role in Türkiye’s engagement with the SCO. Türkiye has participated in cultural exchange programs, promoting mutual understanding and respect among the diverse cultures of the SCO member states. In times of humanitarian need, such as natural disasters, Türkiye has collaborated with SCO countries to provide relief and support to affected populations.


### Balancing International Relations


Türkiye’s involvement with the SCO is carefully balanced with its relationships with other international organizations, such as NATO and the EU. This balancing act allows Türkiye to maintain a unique position where it can act as a bridge between different geopolitical blocs, leveraging its strategic location and diplomatic ties.


### Future Prospects


Looking forward, Türkiye is likely to continue exploring ways to deepen its cooperation with the SCO, potentially seeking more significant influence within the organization. This may involve policy changes or strategic initiatives that align with the SCO’s long-term goals and Türkiye’s national interests.

1. **Historical Context:**
  - [Türkiye’s relationship with the SCO began as an observer in 2012 and evolved to formal dialogue partner status in 2013](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/shanghai-cooperation-organization.en.mfa)[1](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/shanghai-cooperation-organization.en.mfa).
  - Since then, Türkiye actively participated in SCO meetings, summits, and working groups, contributing to regional security discussions.
2. **Security Initiatives:**
  - The SCO focuses on non-traditional security threats, including terrorism, drug trafficking, and extremism.
  - Türkiye actively engages in joint anti-terrorism exercises under the SCO umbrella, demonstrating commitment to regional security.
  - [Intelligence sharing within the Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure (RATS) enhances collective security efforts among SCO member states](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/shanghai-cooperation-organization.en.mfa)[2](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-981-15-4708-9_7).
3. **Economic Projects:**
  - Türkiye’s strategic location makes it a vital transit hub for energy and trade routes.
  - Notable infrastructure projects include:
    - Baku-Tbilisi-Ceyhan (BTC) crude oil pipeline
    - South Caucasus natural gas pipeline (SCP)
    - Trans Anatolian natural gas pipeline (TANAP)
    - TurkStream project
    - [Türkiye-Greece natural gas interconnector (ITG)](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/shanghai-cooperation-organization.en.mfa)[3](https://cadmus.eui.eu/bitstream/handle/1814/76267/NIQ%20Vol%2025%20-%20Issue%204%20-%20December%202023.pdf?sequence=1).
  - [Türkiye’s participation in China’s Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) strengthens economic ties within the SCO framework](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/shanghai-cooperation-organization.en.mfa)[4](https://www.dailysabah.com/business/economy/turkish-exports-to-sco-member-countries-up-85-in-last-5-years).
4. **Comparison with Other Organizations:**
  - **NATO:** Türkiye’s SCO engagement does not jeopardize its ties with NATO. [NATO remains Türkiye’s primary security alliance](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/shanghai-cooperation-organization.en.mfa)[5](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2024/06/joining-brics-comes-limitations-turkey-no-alternative-nato).
  - **EU and BRICS:** While the EU focuses on economic integration, BRICS seeks alternatives to the US dollar. [Türkiye’s dual engagement allows it to balance economic and security interests](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/shanghai-cooperation-organization.en.mfa)[5](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2024/06/joining-brics-comes-limitations-turkey-no-alternative-nato).
5. **Future Benefits and Challenges:**
  - Türkiye’s SCO membership enhances regional influence, trade, and connectivity.
  - [Challenges include balancing relations with NATO and addressing differences with Russia and China](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/shanghai-cooperation-organization.en.mfa)[6](https://www.cacianalyst.org/publications/analytical-articles/item/12958-turkey-and-the-shanghai-cooperation-organization-motives-and-consequences.html).
6. **Cultural and Humanitarian Aspects:**
  - Türkiye’s response to the 2023 earthquakes and collaboration with other SCO member states demonstrate its commitment to humanitarian efforts within the organization.
