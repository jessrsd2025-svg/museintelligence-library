A History of the Ottoman Empire by Douglas Howard — Review

Douglas A. Howard’s “A History of the Ottoman Empire” is a comprehensive guide that delves into the intricate history of one of the most influential empires in world history. The book covers the empire’s full history, from its origins in post-Mongol Eurasia to its dissolution after World War I. Howard adopts a holistic approach, exploring the Ottoman worldview, its formation, and eventual disintegration.

The author emphasizes the significant role of the Ottoman sultans and their extended household, shedding light on the evolution of the empire’s fiscal model and analyzing notable works of Ottoman literature. These literary works highlight aspects of spirituality, the awareness of space and time, and the range of human emotions, alongside themes of migration, violence, disease, and disaster.

Howard’s narrative follows the daily lives of the people, their attitudes towards authority, how they earned their livelihoods, and their sense of humor and beauty. This perspective provides readers with a more nuanced understanding of the Ottoman Empire, beyond the common stereotypes and misconceptions.

The book is not only a valuable resource for those interested in the history of the Ottoman Empire, the Middle East, Islamic history, and Eastern Europe but also serves as an essential textbook for graduate and advanced undergraduate courses. It is well-illustrated with over eighty images, maps, and textboxes, which aid in visualizing and understanding the content.

Readers on platforms like Goodreads have rated the book positively, appreciating its detailed and accessible narrative. Similarly, on Amazon, the book has received commendation for its comprehensive coverage and engaging writing style. The book’s approach to countering stereotypes and introducing a diverse cast of characters, both famous and lesser-known, has been particularly noted for enriching the reader’s understanding of the Ottoman Empire’s complex history.

In conclusion, “A History of the Ottoman Empire” by Douglas A. Howard is a well-researched and engagingly written book that offers a deep dive into the empire’s history. It stands out for its holistic approach and its ability to bring the Ottoman world to life for the reader, making it a recommended read for anyone interested in this pivotal period of history.
