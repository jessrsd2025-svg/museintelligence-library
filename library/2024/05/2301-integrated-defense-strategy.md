The service chiefs “Tank Discussion” highlighted the importance of an integrated approach to national defense.


In response to the multifaceted challenges discussed during the Council on Foreign Relations event on May 13, 2024, the U.S. military service chiefs proposed several strategies to ensure the defense apparatus remains robust and capable of addressing current and future threats. The strategies, as gleaned from the discussions and coverage, include:


1. **Force Design and Modernization**: The military leaders emphasized the need for a force design that is responsive to the capabilities of peer competitors like China. This involves modernizing the force with advanced technologies and weapon systems that can operate across different domains and ranges.
2. **Nuclear Deterrence and Arms Control**: With the changing dynamics of global nuclear capabilities, the U.S. is focused on maintaining a credible nuclear deterrent. The service chiefs also expressed a desire for arms control to play a role in managing the proliferation of nuclear weapons and reducing the risk of escalation.
3. **Strengthening Alliances**: The importance of alliances and partnerships was highlighted, with a focus on enhancing cooperation with allies in the Indo-Pacific and other strategic regions. This strategy aims to build a collective defense posture that can deter aggression and maintain regional stability.
4. **Innovation and Adaptability**: The chiefs discussed the necessity for the armed forces to be innovative and adaptable. This includes integrating new technologies and developing concepts of operations that can keep pace with the rapidly evolving nature of warfare.
5. **Shipbuilding and Maritime Capacity**: Addressing concerns about shipbuilding capacity, especially in comparison to China, the service chiefs acknowledged the need for a larger and more capable navy. This strategy involves not only increasing the number of ships but also ensuring they are equipped with the latest technologies.
6. **Arctic Strategy**: With the Arctic becoming an area of increasing strategic interest, the U.S. Coast Guard’s role in icebreaking and maintaining a presence in the region was discussed. The strategy involves updating and expanding the fleet to ensure the U.S. can protect its interests in the Arctic.
7. **NATO and European Defense**: The strengthening of NATO, partly in response to Russian aggression, was mentioned as a key element of the strategy. The U.S. military is committed to supporting NATO’s collective defense efforts and ensuring the alliance remains a cornerstone of European security.


These strategies represent a comprehensive approach to addressing the challenges faced by the U.S. military in a complex global security environment. The service chiefs’ discussion at the CFR event underscores the ongoing efforts to adapt and prepare the U.S. armed forces for the demands of 21st-century warfare and beyond.
