Both Iran and Russia exploit blockchain’s features to fund proxy warfare, evade sanctions, and maintain strategic advantages. [Addressing these challenges requires international cooperation and robust regulatory frameworks](https://swfound.org/media/207797/future-warfare-and-critical-technologies_feb-2024.pdf)


**Iran** and **Russia** leverage blockchain technologies for proxy warfare and their associated illicit financial activities:


1. **Iran’s Use of Blockchain in Proxy Warfare:**
  - **POLONIUM:** Iran employs cyber proxies like **POLONIUM**. These proxies exploit blockchain technology for several reasons:
    - **Cost-Effectiveness:** Cyber infrastructure is cheaper and more accessible than traditional weaponry.
    - **Plausible Deniability:** Cyber proxies allow Iran to operate with plausible deniability, as their actions are not hindered by geography.
    - [**Anonymity:** Blockchain transactions provide a level of anonymity, making it challenging to trace the flow of funds](https://www.reliaquest.com/blog/polonium-proxy-warfare-and-irans-cyber-strategy/)[1](https://www.reliaquest.com/blog/polonium-proxy-warfare-and-irans-cyber-strategy/).
2. **Russia’s Role and Blockchain Adoption:**
  - **Private Military Companies (PMCs):** Russia supports PMCs like **Wagner**, **Redut**, **Gasprom**, **Patriot**, and **Convoy**. These PMCs create dependencies on Russia for both commercial and military assets.
  - **Resource Access:** In return, Russia gains access to strategic natural resources such as gold, diamonds, and cobalt mines. [These resources enhance Russia’s economic and geopolitical influence](https://www.civilaffairsassoc.org/post/decentralized-warfare-russia-s-use-of-cryptocurrencies-to-fund-private-military-companies)[2](https://www.civilaffairsassoc.org/post/decentralized-warfare-russia-s-use-of-cryptocurrencies-to-fund-private-military-companies).
3. **Blockchain’s Facilitation of Illicit Financial Activities:**
  - **Anonymity:** Blockchain transactions are pseudonymous, allowing actors to move funds without revealing their identities.
  - **Cross-Border Transactions:** Blockchain enables seamless cross-border transactions, bypassing traditional banking channels and regulatory scrutiny.
  - **Smart Contracts:** Smart contracts can automate financial flows, making them difficult to detect or regulate.
  - [**Decentralization:** The decentralized nature of blockchain reduces reliance on centralized financial systems, making it harder to monitor and control](https://swfound.org/media/207797/future-warfare-and-critical-technologies_feb-2024.pdf)
