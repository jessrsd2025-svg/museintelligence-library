National Maritime Strategy: Congress has been working on a comprehensive national maritime strategy to rebuild America’s maritime power. This includes enhancing shipbuilding capacity, supporting the maritime workforce, and ensuring robust U.S.-flag shipping.

National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA): The NDAA has included provisions to reduce sea-time requirements for certain maritime endorsements and strengthen U.S.-flag requirements for cargo preference shipments4. This aims to ensure that U.S.-flag ships are prioritized over foreign vessels.

Jones Act: The Jones Act, which mandates that goods transported between U.S. ports be carried on American-built, American-owned, and American-crewed vessels, continues to be a critical piece of legislation supported by maritime advocates5.
These efforts are crucial for maintaining the U.S.’s maritime strength and ensuring that its maritime forces are well-equipped to handle future challenges.
