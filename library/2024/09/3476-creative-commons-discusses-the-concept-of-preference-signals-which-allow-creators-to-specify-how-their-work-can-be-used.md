Creative Commons discusses the concept of preference signals, which allow creators to specify how their work can be used for AI training. These signals are not legally enforceable but aim to establish new norms for sharing and reuse in the AI space. The article highlights the importance of nuanced approaches to digital sharing, especially given the rapid advancements in generative AI.
