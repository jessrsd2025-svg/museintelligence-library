[Today, the CDAO hosted the](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7242290321861017600/?lipi=urn%3Ali%3Apage%3Ad_flagship3_detail_base%3Bu0NSWegfQ%2F2YDMQrYeq3CQ%3D%3D)[#Advana](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=advana&highlightedUpdateUrns=urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A7244309469138948096) Industry Day at the Hyatt Regency in Reston, VA.

The 1-day event featured collaborative opportunities for industry to participate in the continued execution and development of Advana―the [United States Department of Defense](https://www.linkedin.com/company/deptofdefense/) common platform designed for mission owners, analysts, and data scientists to simplify its business systems. Presentations included an informative keynote from CDAO Dr. Radha Plumb; rousing talks on the Advana mission space and its role in the Open DAGIR approach to scaling data, analytics, and AI capabilities; and insight on opportunities for traditional and nontraditional contractors to participate in ongoing Advana development activities.

Learn more about the Advana Industry Day and the DoD’s efforts to strengthen its partnerships with industry: [https://lnkd.in/eyhqtrJc](https://lnkd.in/eyhqtrJc)
