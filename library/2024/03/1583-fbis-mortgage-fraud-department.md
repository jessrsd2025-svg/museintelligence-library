[The FBI’s Mortgage Fraud Department investigates mortgage fraud through a systematic process that includes gathering evidence, interviewing witnesses and subjects, executing search warrants, and analyzing financial documents](https://www.fbi.gov/how-we-can-help-you/more-fbi-services-and-information/freedom-of-information-privacy-act/department-of-justice-fbi-privacy-impact-assessments/mortgage-fraud)[1](https://www.fbi.gov/how-we-can-help-you/more-fbi-services-and-information/freedom-of-information-privacy-act/department-of-justice-fbi-privacy-impact-assessments/mortgage-fraud). When mortgage guarantors or servicers engage in willful cover-ups of title defects in Deeds of Trust, they are essentially participating in fraudulent activities that can significantly impact the marketability of the deed. [Title defects can range from liens, illegal deeds, or forgeries, to boundary disputes, and these issues can make a property difficult to sell or refinance](https://berlinpatten.com/navigating-title-insurance-defects-a-practical-guide/)[2](https://berlinpatten.com/navigating-title-insurance-defects-a-practical-guide/)[3](https://www.legalmatch.com/law-library/article/defective-titles-or-title-defects.html)[4](https://mylandtrust.com/blog/defects-affect-transfer-of-title/).


Covering up such defects is a breach of legal and contractual obligations, as guarantors and servicers are expected to act in good faith and with transparency. [The concealment of title defects can lead to legal action against the parties involved, including severe penalties, fines, or even imprisonment for those found guilty of bad faith conduct](https://fitterlaw.com/insight/legal-dictionary/define-bad-faith/)[5](https://fitterlaw.com/insight/legal-dictionary/define-bad-faith/). Additionally, such actions undermine the integrity of the mortgage market by eroding trust and potentially causing financial harm to homeowners and investors.


The ramifications of these actions can be far-reaching, affecting not only the immediate parties involved but also the broader financial system. It can lead to increased scrutiny and regulation of mortgage practices, as well as a loss of confidence among consumers and financial institutions. Ultimately, maintaining the integrity of the mortgage market is crucial for the stability of the real estate sector and the economy at large.
