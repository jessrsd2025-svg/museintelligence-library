If you suspect mortgage fraud, there are several avenues for reporting it:


1. **Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA)**:
  - FHFA’s Office of Inspector General handles mortgage fraud reports.
  - [You can **report possible mortgage fraud** targeting you or your mortgage company to FHFA online or by calling **800-793-7724**](https://www.fhfa.gov/PolicyProgramsResearch/Programs/Pages/Fraud-Prevention.aspx)[1](https://www.fhfa.gov/PolicyProgramsResearch/Programs/Pages/Fraud-Prevention.aspx).
2. **Fannie Mae**:
  - Fannie Mae relies on its lender partners and industry members to identify potential mortgage fraud.
  - [If you have information about mortgage fraud, you can **complete and submit the Suspected Mortgage Fraud Report** or call **1-800-2FANNIE (1-800-232-6643)**](https://www.fhfa.gov/PolicyProgramsResearch/Programs/Pages/Fraud-Prevention.aspx)[2](https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/mortgage-fraud-prevention).
  - Fannie Mae also issues **fraud alerts** to keep the industry informed about potential fraudulent scenarios.
3. **Other Reporting Options**:
  - Contact the **FBI** by reaching out to your nearest field office or calling **1-800-225-5324**. You can also report online.
  - The **Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)** accepts tips via its hotline.
  - [Notify necessary financial institutions, lenders, and real estate agents](https://www.businessinsider.com/personal-finance/mortgage-fraud)[3](https://www.businessinsider.com/personal-finance/mortgage-fraud)[4](https://www.wikihow.com/Report-Mortgage-Fraud).
