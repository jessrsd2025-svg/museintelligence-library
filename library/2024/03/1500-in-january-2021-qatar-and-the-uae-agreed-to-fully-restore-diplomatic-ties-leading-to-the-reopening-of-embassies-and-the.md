The diplomacy between the United Arab Emirates (UAE) and Qatar has seen significant developments over the years. Following a years-long rift that began in 2017, the two nations have taken steps to improve relations. The UAE, along with Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, and Egypt, had imposed a blockade on Qatar, largely over its engagement with and support of Islamist groups after the Arab Spring protests in 2011. [These countries viewed such groups as terrorists, including the Muslim Brotherhood](https://apnews.com/article/qatar-uae-gulf-politics-diplomacy-a6fbc6fe55baae6ae0665be3a8872a09)[1](https://apnews.com/article/qatar-uae-gulf-politics-diplomacy-a6fbc6fe55baae6ae0665be3a8872a09).


[However, in January 2021, Qatar and the UAE agreed to fully restore diplomatic ties, leading to the reopening of embassies and the resumption of diplomatic representation](https://apnews.com/article/qatar-uae-gulf-politics-diplomacy-a6fbc6fe55baae6ae0665be3a8872a09)[2](https://www.state.gov/qatar-and-the-united-arab-emirates-restore-diplomatic-ties/). [This reconciliation was part of a broader trend among Gulf Cooperation Council partners to promote regional stability and mutual cooperation](https://apnews.com/article/qatar-uae-gulf-politics-diplomacy-a6fbc6fe55baae6ae0665be3a8872a09)[3](https://bing.com/search?q=United+Arab+Emirates+and+Qatar+diplomacy).


[The reopening of embassies and the restoration of diplomatic relations represent a significant step towards normalizing relations and working together on common interests in the region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar%E2%80%93United_Arab_Emirates_relations)[4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar%E2%80%93United_Arab_Emirates_relations). [The United States has welcomed this development, seeing it as a positive move for regional stability and cooperation among GCC countries](https://www.state.gov/qatar-and-the-united-arab-emirates-restore-diplomatic-ties/)[2](https://www.state.gov/qatar-and-the-united-arab-emirates-restore-diplomatic-ties/).
