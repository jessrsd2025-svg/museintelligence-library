In managing its ties with the US and Hamas, Qatar employs a strategic balancing act. [The US values Qatar for its role in regional security and mediation efforts, including negotiations with Hamas for hostage deals](https://jewishinsider.com/2024/03/blinken-al-thani-qatar-israel-hamas-washington/). [Qatar’s relationship with Hamas is complex; while it provides financial support to the group, it also leverages this connection to mediate between Hamas and other parties, including Israel](https://www.foreignaffairs.com/israel/qatars-balancing-act-gaza-hamas).
