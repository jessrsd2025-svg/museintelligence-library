Endsley’s Cognitive Model of Situation Awareness (SA) is a framework that describes the process of achieving and maintaining situation awareness in dynamic environments. It outlines three levels of SA:


**Perception of the Elements in the Environment**: Recognizing important data and cues in the immediate surroundings.


**Comprehension of the Situation**: Understanding what the perceived information means in relation to the goals and objectives.


**Projection of Future Status**: Anticipating future events and dynamics based on the current situation.


1.


This model is particularly influential in fields where quick and accurate decision-making is critical, such as aviation, military operations, and emergency services. [It emphasizes the importance of not just gathering information, but also interpreting it and predicting what might happen next](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situation_awareness)[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situation_awareness)[2](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/1555343415572631)[3](https://1library.net/article/endsley-model-situation-awareness-situation-awareness-approach-context.yjrrg6mz)[4](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-32-9582-7_2).


[For more detailed information, you can refer to the comprehensive resources available on the topic, which discuss the cognitive processes involved in SA, the factors affecting it, and its application in various domains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situation_awareness)[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situation_awareness)[2](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/1555343415572631)[3](https://1library.net/article/endsley-model-situation-awareness-situation-awareness-approach-context.yjrrg6mz)[4](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-32-9582-7_2).
