In the Red Sea region, Qatar’s influence extends through its diplomatic efforts and economic investments.


Qatar’s dual role in the Persian Gulf and Red Sea regions is characterized by its strategic diplomatic engagements and its efforts to mediate in regional conflicts. [Qatar has positioned itself as a mediator in disputes and has hosted dialogue for various regional issues, maintaining open channels with diverse actors, including those with conflicting interests](https://www.washingtoninstitute.org/policy-analysis/gulf-security-qatari-view)[1](https://www.washingtoninstitute.org/policy-analysis/gulf-security-qatari-view)[2](https://apnews.com/article/world-cup-2022-qatar-diplomacy-3a5eaa658536aab231ea9ea8c4ab99dc)[3](https://www.wusf.org/2024-01-17/the-tiny-persian-gulf-nation-of-qatar-occupies-a-big-role-on-the-world-stage).


In the Persian Gulf, Qatar’s approach to security emphasizes the need for cooperation among Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) members and the resolution of border delineation issues. [Qatar advocates for communication and negotiation over isolation and sanctions, believing that engaging with countries like Iran and Iraq can lead to normalization of relations and peace in the region](https://www.washingtoninstitute.org/policy-analysis/gulf-security-qatari-view)[1](https://www.washingtoninstitute.org/policy-analysis/gulf-security-qatari-view).


In the Red Sea region, Qatar’s influence extends through its diplomatic efforts and economic investments. [It has engaged in initiatives to mediate conflicts and has been involved in discussions regarding regional security and cooperation](https://apnews.com/article/world-cup-2022-qatar-diplomacy-3a5eaa658536aab231ea9ea8c4ab99dc)[2](https://apnews.com/article/world-cup-2022-qatar-diplomacy-3a5eaa658536aab231ea9ea8c4ab99dc).


[Qatar’s role in these regions reflects its broader foreign policy goals of fostering stability and acting as a neutral mediator, while also seeking to expand its global influence](https://www.wusf.org/2024-01-17/the-tiny-persian-gulf-nation-of-qatar-occupies-a-big-role-on-the-world-stage)[3](https://www.wusf.org/2024-01-17/the-tiny-persian-gulf-nation-of-qatar-occupies-a-big-role-on-the-world-stage). The strategic partnership between Qatar and Turkey has strengthened over recent years, based on shared political interests, economic cooperation, and mutual support in regional conflicts. Both nations have supported similar groups in the Middle East, such as the Muslim Brotherhood, and have coordinated their positions on issues like the Syrian conflict. Qatar’s investments in Turkey’s economy, especially during economic downturns, underscore the depth of their relationship. [This alliance serves as a counterbalance to the influence of other regional powers like Saudi Arabia and the UAE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar%E2%80%93Turkey_relations).

Qatar views its relationship with Iran as generally positive and cooperative, especially in comparison to other Persian Gulf monarchies. [Despite some points of contention, the two countries have maintained cordial relations, largely due to their shared economic interests, such as the exploitation of the South Pars/North Dome gas field](http://www.us-iran.org/resources/2022/3/1/media-guide-qatar)[1](http://www.us-iran.org/resources/2022/3/1/media-guide-qatar)[2](https://www.mei.edu/publications/us-iran-qatar-triangle)[3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Qatar_relations). [Qatar has also sought to mediate between Iran and other countries, including the United States, to de-escalate tensions and promote diplomatic solutions](https://www.mei.edu/publications/us-iran-qatar-triangle)[2](https://www.mei.edu/publications/us-iran-qatar-triangle).


The role of women in Qatari society has been evolving, with the government supporting initiatives to increase women’s participation in various sectors. Women in Qatar have achieved educational excellence and hold important positions in fields such as education, health, law, and media. [The nation aims to increase the number of women in decision-making positions and has seen a rise in female participation in the labor force and equal wages in the government sector](http://www.us-iran.org/resources/2022/3/1/media-guide-qatar).


Qatar’s economic diversification faces several challenges, including the need to reduce its reliance on hydrocarbon resources and to develop other sectors like finance, tourism, education, and technology. The blockade imposed by neighboring countries in 2017 highlighted the importance of self-sufficiency and diversifying trading partners. [While the blockade has been lifted, Qatar continues to work on strengthening its non-energy sectors and attracting foreign investment, despite structural challenges such as the lack of fiscal policy diversity and investment in the industrial sector](https://oxfordbusinessgroup.com/reports/qatar/2022-report/economy/better-positioned-diversification-drive-and-strong-fundamentals-support-recovery)[8](https://oxfordbusinessgroup.com/reports/qatar/2022-report/economy/better-positioned-diversification-drive-and-strong-fundamentals-support-recovery)[9](https://www.dohainstitute.org/en/Events/2021-Economic-Diversification-in-the-Gulf-Opportunities-and-Challenges/Pages/index.aspx)[10](https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/10/11/2021/arab-center-to-host-economic-diversification-in-the-gulf-states-opportunities-and-challenges-conference)[11](https://oxfordbusinessgroup.com/reports/qatar/2019-report/economy/determined-to-diversify-the-country-has-doubled-down-on-its-drive-to-broaden-its-economic-bases-and-increase-private-sector-involvement)[12](https://oxfordbusinessgroup.com/reports/qatar/2016-report/economy/a-broader-base-the-government-is-making-efforts-to-diversify-the-economy).
