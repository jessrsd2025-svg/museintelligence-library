ODNI Makes Marie Falkowski Permanent Deputy IC CDO, Deputy ADNI for Data & Partnerships


Marie Falkowski has been officially appointed as the **Deputy IC Chief Data Officer (CDO)** and **Deputy Assistant Director of National Intelligence (ADNI) for Data & Partnerships** at the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI). [She will no longer be in an acting capacity in these roles, which she has been assuming since February](https://executivegov.com/2024/03/odni-makes-marie-falkowski-permanent-deputy-ic-cdo/)[1](https://executivegov.com/2024/03/odni-makes-marie-falkowski-permanent-deputy-ic-cdo/).


Falkowski’s career began in the U.S. Air Force in 1990, and she has since held various significant positions, including at the Department of Energy and the CIA. [Her extensive experience in artificial intelligence, data analytics, and digital innovation makes her a valuable asset for the ODNI](https://executivegov.com/2024/03/odni-makes-marie-falkowski-permanent-deputy-ic-cdo/)[1](https://executivegov.com/2024/03/odni-makes-marie-falkowski-permanent-deputy-ic-cdo/).


[In her new role, Falkowski is responsible for developing and executing data strategies, standards, and policies, integrating advanced technologies like artificial intelligence and machine learning, and overseeing initiatives to expedite technology adoption and innovation within the intelligence community](https://www.hstoday.us/industry/people-on-the-move/marie-falkowski-takes-on-new-role-at-dni/)[2](https://www.hstoday.us/industry/people-on-the-move/marie-falkowski-takes-on-new-role-at-dni/).


**Summary:** Marie Falkowski’s appointment as Deputy IC CDO and Deputy ADNI for Data & Partnerships solidifies her role in enhancing the nation’s strategic advantage through data management and analysis in the intelligence community.
