Iran’s crucial role in the Middle East’s regional instability


Iran plays a significant role in the Middle East’s regional instability, primarily through its support of proxy groups and its “Axis of Resistance.” Despite Iran’s claims of these groups acting independently, its funding, arming, and training of them is a central factor in the geopolitical unrest in the region. The recent escalation of conflict, including attacks on US military installations and the involvement of Iranian-backed groups in various regional conflicts, underscores the volatile situation. [Iran’s strategy of “forward defense” by supporting allied militant groups beyond its borders has been a longstanding approach to counter perceived threats and assert its influence, contributing to its rivalry with Saudi Arabia and tensions with Sunni Arab neighbors](https://www.silobreaker.com/blog/irans-crucial-role-in-the-middle-easts-regional-instability/)[1](https://www.silobreaker.com/blog/irans-crucial-role-in-the-middle-easts-regional-instability/).
