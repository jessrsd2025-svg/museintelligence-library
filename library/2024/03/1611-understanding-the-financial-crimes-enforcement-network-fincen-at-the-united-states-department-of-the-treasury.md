The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) is a bureau of the United States Department of the Treasury. [Its primary role is to collect and analyze information about financial transactions in order to combat domestic and international money laundering, terrorist financing, and other financial crimes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Crimes_Enforcement_Network)[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Crimes_Enforcement_Network). [FinCEN operates both domestically and internationally, working with law enforcement agencies, the regulatory community, and the financial-services community to track suspicious activities and ensure the integrity of the financial system](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/f/fincen.asp)[2](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/f/fincen.asp).

FinCEN and it’s respective roles:


1. **Intelligence Division**: This division is responsible for gathering and analyzing financial data to support enforcement actions and inform regulatory policy.
2. **Enforcement Division**: This division conducts investigations into violations of financial regulations, such as money laundering and terrorism financing.
3. **Regulatory Division**: This division is tasked with developing and enforcing regulations under the Bank Secrecy Act and other laws, and it provides guidance to financial institutions on compliance.
4. **Policy Division**: This division creates strategic policies to address new threats in financial crime and works with other agencies and international partners to enhance anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing (AML/CFT) efforts.
5. **Information Technology Division**: This division supports FinCEN’s mission by developing and maintaining the technological infrastructure necessary for advanced data analytics and information sharing.
6. **Administration Division**: This division handles the administrative aspects of FinCEN, such as human resources, budgeting, and procurement, to ensure smooth operations.


Each division plays a critical role in enabling FinCEN to fulfill its mission of safeguarding the financial system from illicit use and promoting national security through the strategic use of financial intelligence.
