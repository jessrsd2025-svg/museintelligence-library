Handling information overload is a significant challenge for intelligence agencies like the CIA. To manage this, the CIA employs various strategies, such as:


- **Prioritization**: Focusing on the most relevant information based on the current objectives and threats.
- **Collaboration**: Sharing information across different departments and agencies to leverage diverse expertise.
- **Technology**: Utilizing advanced data analytics tools to filter and analyze large datasets effectively.


The CIA’s Technology Fellows program is designed to bring in experts from various fields for one to two years of public service. The program aims to leverage their specialized knowledge to address national security challenges, including foreign technology developments and emerging technologies.
