> Geopolitical and regional factors influencing the Houthi takeover. The Houthi takeover in Yemen, also known as the September 21 Revolution or the 2014–15 coup d’état, was a significant upheaval that had its origins in the Houthi-led protests. The storming of the Yemeni capital Sanaa in September 2014 and the subsequent resignations of Prime Minister Mohammed Basindawa and President Abdrabbuh Mansur Hadi in January 2015 were key events that led to the formation of a ruling council by Houthi militants in February 2015. The UN-brokered deal for a “unity government” was signed on 21 September 2014, but the situation quickly deteriorated, leading to the seizure of the presidential palace and Hadi’s residence by Houthi fighters in January 2015. The Revolutionary Committee was established as the interim authority, but it faced challenges due to the rejection by internal opposition groups and the lack of international recognition12345.
