**WordPress News Aggregator website** is a website that collects and sorts news from different sources into one easy-to-find location. You can aggregate news on your favorite sports team or a certain industry, or just collect general news from different sources. To create a quality WordPress news aggregator website, you’ll want to consider these points:


- Make content sortable.
- Ensure that whatever solution you use to aggregate news lets you add categories to different news sources/articles so that your readers can easily find what they want.
- Aggregate a variety of content.
- Make sure that your site is able to successfully aggregate content from a variety of sources – otherwise, you’re not offering as much benefit to your readers.
- Use a news-friendly theme.
- Choose a WordPress theme that lets visitors quickly scan multiple articles to find one that interests them.
- Make sure you provide attribution.
- You need to provide attribution to the original source so that you don’t end up accidentally stealing content.


Here are the steps you need to take to create a WordPress news aggregator website:


Find a good WordPress host i.e. RSS Feed Pro


1. Pick a theme for your WordPress news aggregator website
2. Install a WordPress RSS aggregator plugin.
3. Find feeds for aggregation.
4. Add feeds to your WordPress aggregator site.
