In high-stakes decentralized finance (DeFi) and geopolitical threat intelligence environments, distinguishing between **Threat Intelligence Signals** (unverified off-chain reporting, Telegram/X exploit leaks, prediction market volatility, or governance proposals) and **Verified On-Chain Compromise** (mismatched storage roots, reentrancy state mutations, unexpected liquidity burns) is essential to prevent systemic market disorientation.

### Epistemic Status Taxonomy for Hybrid Intelligence


1. **Threat Intelligence / Context Signal (`SOURCE_ASSERTION` / `UNVERIFIED`):**
  - *Source Material:* Security researcher blog posts, unverified Discord rumors, zero-day disclosure claims regarding WLFI proxy implementations, Aave v3 pool configuration parameters, or IRGC oil export assertions.
  - *Handling Protocol:* Quarantined in `corridor_claims` or routed to `quarantine.md` / `pending_review.md` as `ALLEGED_BY_SOURCE`. Strictly prohibited from triggering automated pause triggers or protocol interdiction scripts.
2. **Market & Sentiment Signals (`PROBABILISTIC_SIGNAL` / `SENTIMENT_ONLY`):**
  - *Source Material:* Liquidity shifts in WLFI/AAVE DEX pools or Polymarket contract probability spikes.
  - *Handling Protocol:* Processed with `source_use_tag = SENTIMENT_ONLY`. Used exclusively for orientation overlays in the GraphRAG context engine.
3. **Verified Compromise (`VERIFIED_FACT` / `EXECUTION_PROOF`):**
  - *Source Material:* Deterministic RPC block telemetry, state differential proofs, failed invariant checks in formal verification harnesses.
  - *Handling Protocol:* Bound to an immutable SHA-256 state block hash (RFC 8785 canonicalization). Eligible for human-reviewed escalation and automated circuit breaker execution where explicitly authorized.
