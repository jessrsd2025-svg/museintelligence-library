4. Specialized Reasoning Modes


4.1 Spatial Mode (Cartographic & Topological Hygiene)


Spatial Mode governs maritime and infrastructure overlays (e.g., Strait of Hormuz, Persian Gulf, Gulf of Oman, Red Sea, Bab el-Mandeb). It enforces a strict separation between contextual geography and unverified claims (**“Map the Claim, Never Promote the Claim”**): [1]


- **Coordinate Standard:** All polygon and vector geometries must strictly follow WGS84 `[longitude, latitude]` ordering. [1]
- **Navigational Restriction:** Every spatial feature must hardcode `navigation_suitability = 0` to prevent analytical overlays from being treated as operational navigation charts. [1]
- **Visual Layer Semantics:**
  - *White-Hot Geography:* Spatial terrain and baseline infrastructure context; does not establish threat attribution.
  - *Blue Transit Vectors:* Conceptual or historical maritime movement patterns; does not prove vessel identity or C2 coordination.
  - *Red Hollow Nodes:* Unverified source assertions quarantined from causal graph promotion. [1]


4.2 Convergent Mode (Source Decomposition & Legal Baselines)


Convergent Mode prevents cross-source contamination by decomposing composite records into single-origin entities: [1]


1. **`SRC-IRAN-MARINE-ACT-1993` (`municipal_law`):** Isolates literal baseline declarations and domestic maritime zone claims under the *Act on the Marine Areas of the Islamic Republic of Iran*. [1]
2. **`SRC-US-DOS-LIS-114-1994` (`attributed_legal_analysis`):** Isolates U.S. Department of State *Limits in the Seas No. 114* international legal conformity critiques. [1]
3. **Institutional Tokenization Telemetry:** Separates Chainlink CCIP/Proof of Reserve snapshots (`INTEGRITY_ANCHOR`), SDK code examples (`PRIMARY_OFFICIAL` templates), and Aave/WLFI governance proposals (`SOURCE_ASSERTION` co-mentions via `MENTIONED_WITH`). [1]

5. Cryptographic Provenance & OCR Sanitation


To guarantee tamper-evident auditability, every SMCP JSON envelope undergoes deterministic serialization prior to cryptographic hashing: [1]


$$\text{SMCP JSON Envelope} \xrightarrow{\text{OCR Sanitation}} \text{RFC 8785 (JCS)} \xrightarrow{\text{UTF-8 Bytes}} \text{SHA-256 Digest} \longrightarrow \text{Immutable Audit Log}$$ [1]


1. **OCR Token Sanitation:** Corrupted scanner variants (`SOURCE_ASSERATION`, `SOURCE_ASSEPTION`, `SOURCE_ASSERTOIN`, `SOURCE_ASSENTION`, `SOURCE_ASSERCTION`) are intercepted by CI/CD linters and normalized to the canonical schema constant `SOURCE_ASSERTION`. [1]
2. **Duplicate Key Rejection:** JSON parsers reject duplicate object keys (`object_pairs_hook`) prior to canonicalization to eliminate parser-differential ambiguities. [1]
3. **RFC 8785 Canonicalization:** Object keys are sorted lexicographically, IEEE 754 numbers are normalized, and all insignificant whitespace is stripped before computing the 256-bit SHA-256 digest. [1]
