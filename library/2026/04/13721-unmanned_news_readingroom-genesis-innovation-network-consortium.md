**Genesis to Genius Act Innovation Network Consortium** (the Consortium), to be stood up under an **Interagency Strategic Task Force Dynamics** remit within the Office of Science and Technology (OST). The Consortium will operationalize a FedRAMP/IL5‑aligned, GeoJSON/GraphRAG‑backed agentic execution fabric that connects national‑lab discovery to front‑line execution across policy‑bounded corridors.


**Executive summary:** The Consortium will (1) standardize GeoJSON/GraphRAG as the canonical exchange format for corridors, policy snapshots, and attestations; (2) accredit and integrate IL5‑capable agentic orchestration platforms (e.g., n8n FedRAMP Execution Workbench) with mandatory DC Regulatory Spine policy gating; (3) fund and run corridor‑scoped pilots (e.g., VEN‑MENA liquidity monitoring + interdiction) to demonstrate end‑to‑end provenance, compliance, and human‑in‑the‑loop adjudication; and (4) create a governance, oversight, and procurement model that balances mission tempo with civil liberties and auditability.


### 1. Strategic Rationale


The **Interagency Strategic Task Force Dynamics** is the governing body established within the **Office of Science and Technology (OST)** to oversee the operationalization of the **Genesis to Genius Act Innovation Network Consortium**.


### 1. Remit and Governance


The Task Force is responsible for managing the “Agentic Execution Fabric,” a high-security digital architecture designed to transition discovery from U.S. National Laboratories into active, front-line operations.


- **Organizational Home:** It operates under the Office of Science and Technology (OST) with a focus on interagency strategic coordination.
- **Primary Objective:** To connect the **Genesis Mission** (the Department of Energy’s AI-driven research platform) to real-world execution across secure “innovation corridors”.


### 2. The Agentic Execution Fabric


The Task Force operationalizes a specialized technology stack that ensures autonomous AI workflows remain compliant with federal security mandates:


- **FedRAMP/IL5 Alignment:** All operations are strictly aligned with **DoD Impact Level 5 (IL5)** standards, which are required for handling Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) in mission-critical environments.
- **Spatial Intelligence (GeoJSON/GraphRAG):** The Task Force utilizes a **Plan-on-Graph (PoG)** vector database protocol. This architecture maps “RAG chunks” (policy snapshots, lab discovery artifacts, and risk scores) onto geographic nodes for near-real-time situational awareness.
- **Orchestration Nodes:** It leverages the **n8n FedRAMP Execution Workbench** to automate tasks such as **OFAC triggers**, wallet risk scoring, and policy-gated procurement.


### 3. The Genesis Mission Context


The Task Force’s mission is anchored in the **Genesis Mission**, a $293 million Department of Energy initiative launched in early 2026.


- **Lab Integration:** The mission connects 17 national laboratories (including Oak Ridge, Argonne, and Sandia) into a single AI-accelerated discovery platform.
- **Tactical Implementation:** The Task Force ensures that artifacts generated by these labs—such as self-improving AI models or high-integrity data hashes—are used to trigger automated actions within the **Washington-to-state innovation corridors**.


By treating AI agents as **“first-class entities”** with unique identities under **NIST 800-53**, the Task Force maintains an audit-ready decision chain that is compliant with federal mandates and secure against transnational threats.


- **Problem statement:** Federal and mission partners lack a standardized, auditable, and policy‑aware substrate that fuses geospatial topology, provenance, and agentic automation for secure, high‑assurance execution across jurisdictions and financial rails.
- **Opportunity:** National labs are producing attested artifacts and models; modern orchestration platforms can act agentically; GeoJSON/GraphRAG can serve as a canonical, queryable surface. The Consortium will convert discovery into governed action without sacrificing oversight.
- **Strategic outcome:** A resilient, auditable innovation‑to‑execution corridor that preserves policy context with every action and enables rapid, lawful interdiction and mission execution.

### 3. Organizational Design and Governance (How the Consortium Operates)


**3.1 Interagency Strategic Task Force Dynamics (ISTFD)**


- **Charter:** OST establishes the ISTFD to coordinate policy, technical standards, pilots, and oversight across OST, DOE, DoD, DHS, Treasury (FinCEN), and Justice.
- **Structure:** Executive Steering Committee (senior agency reps), Technical Standards Board (GeoJSON/GraphRAG, provenance), Accreditation Office (platform certification), Ethics & Civil Liberties Review Board, and Program Management Office (pilot execution).


**3.2 DC Regulatory Spine (Central Policy Registry)**


- **Function:** A mandatory, authoritative policy registry that publishes policy_snapshot_hash values, enforces gating (FAR/DFARS/OFAC/FinCEN), and provides audit interfaces for oversight bodies.
- **Requirement:** All accredited agentic executions must query and record the Spine’s policy_snapshot_hash before actioning instructions.


**3.3 Roles & Responsibilities**


- **OST:** Standards, interagency coordination, and program oversight.
- **DOE / National Labs:** Produce attested artifacts, model artifacts, and provenance services.
- **DoD / DHS / Treasury:** Operational integration, interdiction authorities, and enforcement playbooks.
- **GSA / TSM:** Procurement lanes and vendor qualification.
- **Private Sector Partners:** Provide accredited tooling, analytics, and enclave services under matching‑fund incentives.


### 4. Technical Architecture & Standards (What to Build)


**4.1 Canonical Data Model: GeoJSON/GraphRAG Hybrid**


- **GeoJSON projection:** Corridors (LineStrings/Polygons), nodes (Points), and features with properties: `corridor_id`, `security_tier`, `rag_tags`, `graph_role`, `policy_snapshot_hash`, `provenance_hash`, `risk_score`, `allowed_event_types`, `required_tokens`, `throughput_limits`, `audit_sinks`.
- **GraphRAG canonical graph:** Property graph (Neo4j‑style) with nodes (corridor, liquidity_node, lab, policy_artifact, instruction, attestation, receipt) and edges (GATED_BY_POLICY, SETTLES_VIA, FLOWS_TO, LAB_TO_EXECUTION). Shared IDs and provenance across both projections.


**4.2 Agentic Orchestration & Identity**


- **Agent as first‑class entity:** Unique identities, AC/IA controls, and audit logging per NIST SP 800‑53 Rev5.
- **Workbench integration:** Accredited n8n/Bluescape/OpenClaw instances operate in IL5 enclaves; tool calls (e.g., `blockchain.analyze_corridor`, `geo.render_geojson`, `fedramp.verify_compliance`) must reference corridor_id and policy_snapshot_hash.


**4.3 Policy Snapshot & Provenance**


- **Policy snapshot hash:** Immutable identifier for the exact policy state; must be attached to every instruction and receipt.
- **Provenance bundles:** Policy snapshot, attestation, receipt, clearance metadata, and evidence anchors (e.g., GDP hash, on‑chain attestations).


**4.4 Security & Enclave Models**


- **Deployment models:** Private cloud, on‑premises, air‑gapped enclaves for IL5 operations.
- **Authentication:** CAC/PIV gating; multi‑factor for human adjudicators.
- **Auditability:** Immutable decision chains, replayable logs, and independent audit interfaces.
