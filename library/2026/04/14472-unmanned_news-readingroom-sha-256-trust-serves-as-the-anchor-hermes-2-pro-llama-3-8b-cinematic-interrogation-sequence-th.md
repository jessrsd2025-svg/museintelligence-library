The **Unmanned_News Reading Room**| Strategic Briefing: The Supra Substrate has moved beyond the era of “forensic reconstruction.” By requiring **RFC-8785 canonicalization** and **SHA-256 hashing** at the point of settlement, we have replaced the “horsehair” of adversarial opacity with a **cryptographic steel chain**


The origins of SHA-256 can be traced back to intelligence services in the US, namely the
National Security Agency, or the NSA for short. Engineers employed at the agency invested
heavily in developing the algorithm, and first published it in 2001. The primary objective of
creating SHA-256 was to optimize earlier hashing functions like MD5 and SHA-1, which
were found to be vulnerable to several known attack vectors.
