## Entry Point to the LIBERTAS Intelligence Lattice v6.10


The **UNMANNED_NEWS ReadingRoom** functions as the operational entry point into the **LIBERTAS Intelligence Lattice v6.10**, providing a governed analytical workflow that moves information through six controlled layers—from observation through reasoning, contextualization, verification, human oversight, and deterministic execution.


Rather than treating incoming information as established fact, the architecture treats every observation as a claim that must accumulate provenance, contextual evidence, and policy validation before it may advance.


---


# 1. OBSERVE


### Collection & Provenance


The Observe layer ingests only authorized information sources while preserving origin metadata and provenance.


Supported inputs include:


- GeoJSON and approved spatial layers
- Open-source intelligence (OSINT) documents and media
- GitHub repositories pinned to immutable commit SHAs
- Authorized sensor and telemetry feeds
- Prediction-market observations explicitly tagged:


```
PROBABILISTIC_SIGNALSENTIMENT_ONLY
```


Prediction-market data remains isolated as market sentiment and is prohibited from becoming factual evidence without additional review.


---


# 2. VERITAS Reasoning Layer


### Adaptive Cognitive Reasoning


VERITAS applies the **Chain of Mindset (CoM)** framework to dynamically select reasoning modes appropriate to each analytical subtask.


The architecture identifies four complementary reasoning modes:


- Divergent (creative exploration)
- Convergent (analytical synthesis)
- Skeptical (adversarial challenge)
- Algorithmic (deterministic execution)


Reasoning remains bounded by the Bidirectional Context Gate so that adaptive cognition never overrides provenance or policy constraints.


---


# 3. GraphRAG Context Engine


### Evidence-Centered Context Assembly


GraphRAG combines:


- Neo4j property graph traversal
- Vector retrieval
- Entity resolution
- Evidence-chain navigation
- GeoFeature linkage
- Policy association


The objective is to connect documents, actors, locations, observations, and evidence while preserving complete lineage rather than generating unsupported relationships.


---


# 4. Multi-Agent Review Mesh


### Structured Analytical Review


Before analytical products advance, specialized reviewers independently evaluate the proposed output.


The architecture defines reviewers for:


- **Planner** — task decomposition and execution planning
- **Critic** — challenges assumptions and inconsistencies
- **Provenance Reviewer** — validates source lineage
- **Security Reviewer** — evaluates permissions and mutation risk
- **Policy Reviewer** — enforces governance constraints
- **Independent/Human Review** — authorizes sensitive transitions


This review mesh is followed by a four-way failure arbitration process that classifies issues as BUG, SPEC_GAP, NOISE, or CONTRACT_AMBIGUITY before escalation to human review.


---


# 5. AEQUITAS Verification Gate


### Silent-Upgrade Interdiction


AEQUITAS operates as the architecture’s deterministic verification firewall.


Its role is to prevent unsupported epistemic promotion, including transitions such as:


- Source Assertion → Verified Fact
- Model Score → Evidence Confidence
- Semantic Similarity → Corroboration
- Market Probability → Event Occurrence
- Actor Mention → Identity Resolution
- Spatial Proximity → Operational Presence
- Influence Corridor → Command-and-Control Relationship
- Generated Imagery → Source Geography


The document further specifies that conceptual representations of organizations such as the Quds Force, Basij, or regional non-state actors must remain **conceptual influence corridors** rather than being interpreted as verified command-and-control structures unless independently established through reviewed evidence.


---


# 6. ACT Controller


### Deterministic Execution


Only outputs that have satisfied governance requirements proceed to execution.


The ACT Controller:


- verifies execution mode
- enforces tool allowlists
- records execution identifiers
- records action hashes
- preserves rollback metadata
- emits schema-valid artifacts
- prevents unauthorized graph or vector mutations
- requires human approval where specified


Execution therefore represents a controlled transformation stage rather than autonomous operational action.


---


# Governance Principles


The uploaded document emphasizes several architectural invariants:


- Every observation begins as a claim requiring provenance.
- Adaptive reasoning supports analysis but does not establish truth.
- Prediction-market information is restricted to sentiment orientation and may not be promoted directly into factual conclusions.
- Human review is required for sensitive promotions, graph relationship registration, prediction-market vectorization, and deployment.
- Governance precedence places human policy and repository controls above reasoning-model preferences.
