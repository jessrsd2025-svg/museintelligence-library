## 2. Seven‑Layer Harness (Conceptual)

1. **Source Contract Layer**
   - Encodes ingestion contracts (`libertas.source-package.v1`, `derived-markdown.v1`, `retrieval-chunk.v1`).
   - Anchors arXiv IDs, version labels, categories, and licensing.

2. **Extraction & Layout Layer**
   - Performs PDF layout analysis and text extraction.
   - Preserves span‑level copyability and locators (sections, pages, equations, tables).

3. **Markdown & Locator Layer**
   - Converts PDFs to provenance‑bearing Markdown.
   - Maintains structural locators and equation/table mappings.

4. **Vector & Retrieval Layer**
   - Indexes chunks in Vectorize or equivalent.
   - Provides semantic retrieval with explicit notice: similarity is **retrieval relevance**, not corroboration.

5. **Graph Lineage Layer**
   - Writes lineage into Neo4j via restricted mutation service.
   - Separates `Source`, `MarkdownArtifact`, `Chunk`, `Claim`, `Evidence`, `ReviewDisposition`, `MarketSignal`.

6. **AEQUITAS Governance Layer**
   - Implements Silent‑Upgrade Interdiction Gate.
   - Blocks transitions from assertion, similarity, market sentiment, or model confidence to verified fact.

7. **Human Review & Publication Layer**
   - Applies scoped dispositions (ACCEPTED, QUARANTINED, REJECTED).
   - Controls repository publication and epistemic promotion.

---

## 3. Harness Properties

- **Contract‑Driven Production:** Every artifact is tied to a schema and contract version.
- **Two‑Pass Compilation:** First pass builds structural artifacts; second pass applies governance and review.
- **Persistent Markdown Memory:** Markdown is stored as a durable, derived representation with locators.
- **Role‑Separated Verification:** Agents and reviewers have distinct responsibilities and restrictions.
- **Four‑Way Failure Classification:** Technical, epistemic, security, and policy failures are separated.
- **Human Arbitration:** Final authority rests with human reviewers.
- **Outer‑Loop Calibration:** System behavior is evaluated against acceptance criteria and doctrinal invariants.

The harness ensures that:

- PDFs remain source objects.
