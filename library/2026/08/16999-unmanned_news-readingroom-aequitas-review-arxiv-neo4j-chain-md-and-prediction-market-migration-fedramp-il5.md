### Prediction-Market Ingestion


Prediction-market observations must be classified as **PROBABILISTIC_SIGNAL**, with an evidence status of **SENTIMENT_ONLY**.


#### Interpretive Limits


Probability, price, volume, and liquidity values must not be treated as establishing any of the following:


- Event occurrence
- Evidence confidence
- Corroboration
- Intent
- Attribution
- Operational presence


#### Prohibited Actions and Relationships


Automated trading and trade recommendations are prohibited. Market signals must not create any of the following relationships:


- **OBSERVED_AT**
- **EVENT_OCCURRED**
- **CONTROLS**
- **TARGETS**
- **COMMANDS**
- **RESPONSIBLE_FOR**

Market signals may be linked only through the following relationships:


- **DERIVED_FROM**
- **REFERENCES**
- **THEMATICALLY_SIMILAR_TO**
- **REQUIRES_REVIEW**


Any proposed relationship that exceeds these limits must be quarantined for review.
