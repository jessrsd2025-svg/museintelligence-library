# OODA Loop in Defense Intelligence and Modern Warfare


The **OODA loop** — *Observe, Orient, Decide, Act* — is a decision-making model developed by U.S. Air Force Colonel **John Boyd** in the early 1970s to describe the cyclical process of combat operations [Wikipedia**+1**](https://www.bing.com/ck/a?!&&p=df632c4ef9f36f620f7adc0c370588db5a5bc644f8fb37b2a3b876a30a4e5426JmltdHM9MTc4NjgzODQwMA&ptn=3&ver=2&hsh=4&fclid=2fb0d465-0456-6374-2184-c3ce057f620c&u=a1aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvT09EQV9sb29w&ntb=1). In military terms, the goal is to **execute the loop faster than an opponent**, thereby “getting inside” their decision cycle and gaining advantage [Wikipedia](https://www.bing.com/ck/a?!&&p=df632c4ef9f36f620f7adc0c370588db5a5bc644f8fb37b2a3b876a30a4e5426JmltdHM9MTc4NjgzODQwMA&ptn=3&ver=2&hsh=4&fclid=2fb0d465-0456-6374-2184-c3ce057f620c&u=a1aHR0cHM6Ly9lbi53aWtpcGVkaWEub3JnL3dpa2kvT09EQV9sb29w&ntb=1).
