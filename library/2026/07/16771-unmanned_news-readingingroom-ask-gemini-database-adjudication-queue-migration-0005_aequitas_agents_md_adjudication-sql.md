## II. Tri-Framework Governance Mechanics & The Silent-Upgrade Interdiction Gate


### 1. LIBERTAS (Spatial & Narrative Cartography)


- **Domain Focus:** Theater-layer modeling, maritime corridor tracking, energy transit flows, and anchor geography.

- **Spatial Integral Form:** $$\mathcal{S}_{\text{state}} = \iint_{\text{Polygon}} \left( \nabla \times \mathbf{F}_{\text{telemetry}} \right) \cdot d\mathbf{A}$$


### 2. VERITAS (Epistemic Provenance Integrity)


- **Domain Focus:** Claim decomposition, source typing, locator tracking, and confidence classification.
- **Operational Posture:** Evaluates all input statements as unverified claims. Separates source assertions from analytical inferences and model scores.
- **Epistemic Rule:** Model confidence scores (e.g., $0.87$) are classified as `MODEL_SCORE` and explicitly tagged with `is_evidence_confidence: false`.


### 3. AEQUITAS (The Silent-Upgrade Interdiction Gate)


- **Domain Focus:** Edge intercept, prohibited-relationship blocking, silent-upgrade interdiction, and human-in-the-loop queueing.
- **Silent-Upgrade Interdiction Rule:** AI agents frequently attempt to “silently upgrade” conceptual, text-based influence descriptions into verified command-and-control ($C2$) or financial edges. ## Chain of Mindset: Reasoning with Adaptive Cognitive Modes


### Overview


The Chain of Mindset (CoM) framework is a training-free agentic reasoning paradigm that enables step-level adaptive mindset orchestration for Large Language Models (LLMs). It dynamically selects among four functionally heterogeneous mindsets: Spatial, Convergent, Divergent, and Algorithmic.


### Key Components


1. **Meta-Agent**:
  - Acts as the central controller, orchestrating reasoning by selecting mindsets, generating call instructions, and internalizing intermediate insights.
2. **Mindsets**:
  - **Spatial Mindset**: Bridges abstract logic and intuitive perception through visual externalization.
  - **Convergent Mindset**: Addresses information overload by constructing a focused reasoning environment.
  - **Divergent Mindset**: Breaks reasoning deadlocks through structured parallel exploration.
  - **Algorithmic Mindset**: Addresses limitations of language models in precise calculation through a code-based generate-execute-repair loop.
3. **Context Gate**:
  - Implements bidirectional semantic filtering between the Meta-Agent and Mindsets.
  - **Input Gate**: Filters relevant context for mindset execution.
  - **Output Gate**: Distills verbose mindset output into concise results for the main chain.
