2. **Critic Agent**: Identifies risks, contradictions, and unsupported assumptions.
3. **Policy Agent**: Checks compliance with applicable constraints and regulations.
4. **Security Agent**: Evaluates access, authorization, and operational safeguards.
5. **Provenance Agent**: Validates the lineage and source sufficiency of evidence.
6. **Cost/Latency Agent**: Assesses operational efficiency, resource cost, and latency.
7. **Human Review Agent**: Routes high-risk or uncertain decisions for analyst oversight.

3. **Critic Review**: The Critic Agent reviews plans for risks and assumptions.

6. **Provenance Validation**: The Provenance Agent verifies the evidence lineage.
7. **Consensus Scoring**: The system scores the consensus among agents.
