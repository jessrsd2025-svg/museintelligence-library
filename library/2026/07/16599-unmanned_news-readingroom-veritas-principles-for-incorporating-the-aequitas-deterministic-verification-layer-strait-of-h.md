**# LIBERTAS v6.10 — Epistemic Status and Silent-Upgrade Detection**


**## Mission**


Prevent claims, observations, relationships, geometries, and assessments from being


silently promoted to a stronger epistemic state without sufficient evidence, provenance,


authorization, and review.


The layer enforces two controls:


1. Every material knowledge object has a mandatory `epistemic_status`.


2. Every status change is checked for a silent or unsupported upgrade.


**## Model assignments**


**### GPT-5.6 Sol — VERITAS provenance steward**


`GPT-5.6_Sol` prepares provenance-preserving analytical objects and must retain:


– Source attribution


– Exact source locators


– Canonicalization records


– Evidence lineage


– Transformation history


– Contradicting evidence


– Alternative hypotheses


– Uncertainty


– Reproducibility metadata


– Human-review state


GPT-5.6 Sol may propose a status transition. It cannot authorize its own proposal.


**### Llama 4 Maverick — AEQUITAS deterministic verification layer**


`Llama_4_Maverick` examines the proposed transition package and produces a typed


verification finding.


Its output is advisory until deterministic rules validate the finding.


It may:


– Compare before-and-after objects.


– Detect omitted qualifiers.


– detect attribution changes.


– identify unsupported confidence increases.


– identify missing source locators.


– detect source-to-assessment laundering.


– recommend `PASS`, `BLOCK`, or `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.


It may not:


– Perform the final state mutation.


– invent evidence.


– replace deterministic validation.


– waive policy or provenance requirements.


– convert model agreement into corroboration.


**## Governing principle**


> No epistemic status may improve merely because text was summarized, retrieved,


> normalized, mapped, repeated, or accepted by another model.

**# Mandatory `epistemic_status`**


**## Controlled values**


| Status | Meaning |


|—|—|


| `SOURCE_ASSERTION` | A statement attributed to a source |


| `UNVERIFIED_OBSERVATION` | A normalized observation not yet verified |


| `PARTIALLY_CORROBORATED` | Some independent support exists, but material gaps remain |


| `CORROBORATED` | Independent evidence supports the material claim |


| `CONTESTED` | Credible supporting and contradicting evidence coexist |


| `ANALYTICAL_INFERENCE` | A conclusion derived from evidence and stated assumptions |

| `REJECTED` | Failed evidentiary or analytical review |


| `SUPERSEDED` | Replaced by a later object without erasing history |

**# Silent-upgrade definition**


A ****silent upgrade**** occurs when an object is represented as more certain, more verified,


more directly attributed, or more authoritative than its preceding state without an explicit,


validated transition record.
