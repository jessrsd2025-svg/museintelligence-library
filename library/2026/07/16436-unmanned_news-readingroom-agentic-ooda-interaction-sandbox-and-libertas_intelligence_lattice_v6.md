**Strategic Cartography Principle**


The Intelligence Cartography Core models knowledge as an interconnected graph rather than a collection of isolated reports. Geographic features, infrastructure, documents, observations, and assessments are linked through explicit relationships that preserve analytical context, provenance, and review history.


This structure allows every visualization, query, and published assessment to be audited, reproduced, and traced through a transparent chain of evidence while supporting iterative, human-supervised OODA cycles within a GraphRAG-enabled knowledge environment.


**Core Relationship Groups**


- **Observation relationships:** OBSERVED_AT links observations to spatial or temporal context; DERIVED_FROM records analytical transformation from observation to feature; LOCATED_IN associates entities with geographic regions.
- **Evidence relationships:** SUPPORTED_BY connects assessments to supporting evidence, while RELATED_TO captures semantic associations between graph objects.
- **Infrastructure relationships:** IMPLEMENTS maps policy or doctrine to infrastructure capability, and REFERENCES links documents to observations, evidence, or graph objects.
- **Provenance relationships:** CANONICALIZED_AS, DIGESTED_BY, SIGNED_BY, and REGISTERED_TO preserve the chain from original observation to canonical object, cryptographic digest, signature, and repository registration.
- **Review and lineage relationships:** REVIEWED_BY records human validation, REQUIRES_REVIEW routes uncertain assessments for additional evaluation, and EXTENDS_LINEAGE maintains historical continuity across evidence objects.
- **Agentic OODA relationships:** ORIENTED_WITH connects observations to contextual intelligence; PROPOSED_PLAN represents analytical options; VALIDATED_BY records review of recommendations; EXECUTED_AS captures implementation status; and PRODUCED_FEEDBACK returns outcomes into the next observation cycle.


**Agentic OODA Graph Flow**


The OODA graph links the provenance chain to the decision cycle: observations are canonicalized, digested, signed, and registered before they orient the knowledge graph; decisions generate proposed plans; validated actions are executed; and feedback returns new evidence to the Observe phase.

**Layer V — Agentic OODA Interaction Sandbox**


The Agentic OODA Interaction Sandbox structures the analytical workflow as an iterative Observe–Orient–Decide–Act cycle. It is designed to let analysts test how multi-agent reasoning, provenance verification, and human oversight work together before an assessment or action is advanced.


**OODA Workflow**


The sandbox follows a repeatable loop in which observations are gathered, contextualized, evaluated, acted on, reviewed, and then folded back into the knowledge base.


1. **Observe:** Ingest events, source metadata, timestamps, and initial evidence markers.
2. **Orient:** Apply GraphRAG context, entity resolution, vector retrieval, policy constraints, and provenance checks.
3. **Decide:** Compare candidate interpretations or plans through proposer, critic, validator, security, provenance, and human-review agents.
4. **Act:** Execute only validated intent through deterministic, auditable components.
5. **Review:** Examine outputs, side effects, rejected paths, and analyst feedback.
6. **Knowledge Update:** Extend graph lineage, preserve review state, and feed verified changes back into the knowledge fabric.


**Agent Roles**


- **Geospatial Analysis:** Interprets spatial relationships, locations, routes, and infrastructure context.
- **Temporal Correlation:** Aligns events across timelines, phases, and sequence patterns.
- **Entity Resolution:** Reconciles names, identifiers, organizations, locations, and observed objects across sources.
- **Graph Reasoning:** Expands relationships, retrieves relevant context, and supports explainable inference.
- **Provenance Verification:** Checks lineage, source attribution, canonicalization, and review status before claims are promoted.
- **Human Review Coordination:** Routes uncertain, high-impact, or policy-sensitive outputs to analyst oversight.


**Purpose and Safeguards**


The sandbox supports experimentation with multi-agent analytical workflows while keeping analyst oversight at the center. It helps teams test how latency, graph density, provenance quality, semantic drift, and review thresholds affect loop stability, orientation confidence, and cascade-risk exposure.


The governing principle remains: reports are not treated as truth by default; they are treated as claims that require traceable lineage before they inform knowledge or action.

*Renderings of Strategic Dynamics* defines a conceptual analytical environment that brings together geospatial observations, infrastructure data, documentary evidence, and graph relationships within a provenance-aware knowledge fabric.


The framework supports transparent, explainable analysis through standardized data transformation, cryptographic lineage, and human-centered review. Cartography is treated not only as a representation of places, but also as a representation of evidence: analysts can trace how observations become relationships, how those relationships support knowledge, and how each assessment remains linked to documented sources.


**Multi-Agent Consensus Model**


Decision-making is structured as a controlled contest among specialized agents. Each agent examines a proposed action from a distinct analytical, operational, or governance perspective before the system advances toward consensus.


- **Proposer Agents:** Generate candidate plans for evaluation.
- **Critic Agents:** Identify risks, gaps, assumptions, and likely failure modes.
- **Policy Agents:** Check proposed actions against applicable constraints.
- **Security Agents:** Evaluate authorization, access conditions, and operational safeguards.
- **Provenance Agents:** Validate lineage, source attribution, and traceability.
- **Cost/Lag Agents:** Assess efficiency, resource cost, and latency.
- **Human Review Agents:** Escalate high-risk or uncertain actions for analyst oversight.
- **Consensus Engine:** Selects, rejects, or returns plans for revision.


**Consensus Flow**


Structured Context → Candidate Plans → Critic Review → Policy Validation → Security Validation → Provenance Validation → Consensus → Human Interrupt → Validated Intent
